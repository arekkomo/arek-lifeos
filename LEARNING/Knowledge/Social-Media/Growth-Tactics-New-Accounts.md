# One More: growth from zero

## The launch objective

The first 30 posts are a **learning system**, not a follower race. Aim to discover which food/place/host story produces saves, shares, profile visits and follows among people near Vancouver and the Sunshine Coast. Keep a simple weekly scorecard:

`follow conversion = follows from post ÷ accounts reached × 100`

`engagement by reach = (likes + comments + saves + shares) ÷ accounts reached × 100`

Do not compare these early values with large established accounts; compare one One More post against another and document the hook, format, time, audio, location tag, CTA and outcome.

## 30-day organic playbook

### Before first post

- Switch to an Instagram **professional account**, make bio searchable (“curated meals + stays | Sunshine Coast, BC”), add a working booking/waitlist link, email and location.
- Seed a nine-post grid before outreach: 3 food, 3 process/people, 3 place/experience. Pin three posts: what One More is, strongest dish Reel, and destination/guest promise.
- Build a collaborator list: local growers, fishers, makers, accommodation partners, cafes, tourism/community accounts and 20 genuine potential guests. Ask permission before tagging or sharing.

### Every week

- Publish the 4-post launch schedule in [[Posting-Strategy]]. Start Reels in week 1: one discovery Reel is the smallest viable test; aim for two weekly by week 3 if production quality remains high.
- Spend 15–20 minutes, four days/week, leaving **specific, non-promotional** comments on local makers, community venues and food creators. Respond to every meaningful comment and DM within one business day.
- Use Collab posts only with real participants (maker, chef, host, tourism/venue partner). A collaboration should give both audiences a truthful reason to care.
- Turn one meal into a cross-promotion sequence: Reel = the dish; carousel = source/recipe detail; Story = road/ferry/light/room; partner Collab = the maker/place; pinned highlight = “Coast.” This links food to destination without pretending accommodation is already bookable.
- Ask customers and friends for permission to reshare candid stories; do not script praise.

## Engagement pods: do not use

Engagement pods, purchased followers, follow/unfollow routines and automated likes/comments create low-quality signals and can violate platform rules. They also contaminate the account’s own learning: a post can appear “engaged” while reaching no likely guest. Meta's developer materials distinguish permitted publishing/comment-management capabilities from automated inauthentic interaction; keep automation to approved account operations, not simulated human engagement. [Meta, Instagram Platform Overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/overview/)

**Replacement:** build a small “local signal network” of genuine collaborators. Comment because the post is relevant, tag only with consent, and use shared posts where the audience overlap is real.

## Reels: production rules

- **Frequency:** 1/week in weeks 1–2; 2/week from week 3 when the edit buffer allows; 1–2/week steady state.
- **Structure:** visible food or place in first second → one action → finished payoff → one low-friction CTA.
- **Duration:** start with 15–35 seconds. Measure retention, replays, shares, profile visits and follows; do not optimize to a generic duration number.
- Prioritize natural kitchen sound, voice, and clear visual continuity. Use trend audio only if it fits the brand and is commercially usable.
- Publish the same core scene as a still/carousel only when it tells a different story; do not duplicate-feed dump.

## What “working” looks like after 30 posts

Choose the next month’s mix from evidence:

- A format is a **discovery winner** when it has top-quartile non-follower reach and follow conversion.
- A format is a **consideration winner** when it has top-quartile saves/shares or DMs.
- A destination post is useful if it sends profile visits, link taps or place questions, even with fewer likes.
- Pause a repeated format if it has been tested three times and stays bottom-quartile on the metric it was meant to earn.
