# One More: posting strategy

## Operating decision

Do not manufacture volume. With two new dishes per week, turn every dish into a small asset set: one discovery Reel, one saveable carousel, two to four Stories, and optional detail still. Publish **4 feed posts per week for the first 30 days**, then **3–4 per week** once the back catalogue is thin. This sits inside Buffer's 2026 3–5-post weekly range, where its two-million-post analysis found the largest reach-per-post step occurs when accounts move from 1–2 to 3–5 posts weekly. [Buffer, 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)

### First 30 days: practical calendar

| Weekly slot | Asset | Purpose | Source material |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tue, 13:00–16:00 PT | 15–35 sec Reel | non-follower discovery | dish reveal / heat / plating motion |
| Wed, 12:00–14:00 PT | 4–7-slide carousel | saves and story | ingredient → process → finished plate |
| Thu, 12:00–14:00 PT | Reel or photo | personality / place | Robert or Arek, kitchen, Sunshine Coast light |
| Sun, 17:00–19:00 PT | carousel or photo | local weekend intent | table, stay, menu teaser; test rather than assume |

The weekday times are a **starting test window**, not a food-specific guarantee: Sprout's March 2026 global analysis of nearly two billion engagements found strongest overall periods Monday 14:00–16:00, Tuesday 13:00–19:00, Wednesday 12:00–21:00, and Thursday 12:00–14:00; Tuesday/Wednesday peaked highest and weekends were lowest overall. Use PT because the buyer is Vancouver/Sunshine Coast, then replace these defaults with Insights after 30 posts. [Sprout Social, 2026](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-instagram/)

### Steady state

- **3 feed posts/week:** 1–2 Reels, 1 carousel, 0–1 hero still.
- **Stories:** 3–5 frames on each feed day; one informal Story sequence on one non-feed day. Stories serve existing followers and DMs, not the primary discovery engine.
- Keep a two-week buffer: 8 feed posts prepared before launch, then replenish four assets per dish.
- No more than one dish-led feed post per day. Let each format collect its own signal.

## Format mix

Use a **45 / 35 / 20 split** across a 20-post sample:

- **9 Reels (45%)** — sensory motion, chef/host presence, arrival-to-plate mini-narrative. Start in week 1; there is no strategic reason to wait.
- **7 carousels (35%)** — the most useful format for a meal: sequence, ingredient provenance, menu or stay guide. Design slide 1 to stand alone in grid.
- **4 single images (20%)** — only unmistakable hero images, a table invitation, or a destination frame; not filler.

This is a launch hypothesis to A/B test, not a universal platform rule. Buffer's frequency evidence covers photos, Reels, and carousels together, so format allocation must be decided from One More's own reach, saves, shares, profile visits, and follows per post. [Buffer, 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)

## Hashtag system

Use **5–8 specific hashtags**, placed at the end of the caption. Rotate only after tracking results; do not paste a 30-tag block or use engagement bait.

| Layer | Count | Examples — validate current local usage before publishing |
|---|---:|---|
| Brand | 1 | `#OneMoreVan` (choose and lock one canonical tag) |
| Place | 2 | `#SunshineCoastBC`, `#VancouverFood` |
| Experience | 1–2 | `#BCGetaway`, `#SunshineCoast` |
| Dish/craft | 1–3 | specific ingredient, technique, or menu format |

Hashtags are retrieval context, not a reach lever in isolation. Put searchable place and dish language in the first caption sentence and alt text; retain a sheet with each tag, post, reach, follows, saves, and shares.

## Weekly production handoff

1. On shoot day, name assets `YYYY-MM-DD_dish_format_hook_v01`.
2. Connector selects one Reel, one carousel and one still; drafts captions from [[Caption-Guidelines]].
3. Schedule only after an owner reviews the actual crop, audio, location wording and CTA.
4. At 48 hours log reach, non-follower reach, plays, average watch time, saves, shares, profile visits, follows and DMs.
5. Each Monday, keep the best hook/format and retire the weakest recurring pattern after at least three tests.
