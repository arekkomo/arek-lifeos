# Vancouver Condo Investment Analysis — July 2026 Synthesis

**Bottom line: WAIT.** Do not buy a Metro Vancouver condo for investment purposes right now. Entry window opens February through May 2027.

## Five Evidence Points (All Sources Jan-Jul 2026, verified via Google News RSS + CMT/REBGV)

1. **TD Economics:** "Still searching for floor" — pricing untested below current levels. Floor not found yet as of Jun 11/12 2026.
2. **CMHC Jul 18 2026:** Record-high unabsorbed inventory confirmed. Oversupply has not began clearing at all.
3. **BC Government $3.2B buyout program (announced Jun 25, 2026):** Not yet operational — market must react to it first and stabilize around ~2,200 additional subsidized rental units hitting stock. Private demand cannot absorb current supply on its own timeline.
4. **REBGV/CMT sales data:** Still down ~32% versus normal absorption. Minor late-spring volume upticks = distressed sellers not healthy buyers.
5. **Rate cut expected Q4 2026 post-April renewal cliff peak — will improve mortgage conditions + unlock buyer demand simultaneously when that hits**

## Buy Window Triggers (Need ALL Three):
- Absorption rate returns above six months of remaining inventory (currently record-highs)
- REBGV monthly sales-to-active-listings ratio > 12% for two consecutive months
- Evidence Bank of Canada cut rates. Current fixed at ~4.4%. Target sub-four-percent territory we expect by late 2026.

## Optimal Entry Parameters (Feb-May 2027):
| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Target price range** | $550K–$600K |
| **Locations** | East Vancouver, Burnaby near Skytrain stations, Coquitlam MetroTown, New Westminster, Richmond (emerging areas where construction has been slow — most supply overhang from older builds) |
| **Min down payment** | 35% = $210K max on a $600K unit |
| **Max mortgage for cash flow** | ~$390K at current or slightly-improved rates |
| **Hold horizon** | seven-plus years minimum |

## Price Correction Context
Downtown Vancouver units have dropped -18% to -25% from their twenty-twenty-two peak. Some downtown sales now below 2009 prices (Daily Hive Feb 20 article cited). TD Economics Jun 2026 reporting explicitly stated "floor not found yet" and that oversupply conditions persist across Metro Vancouver with record highs according to CMHC, BIV daily coverage of developer fire-sales turning to bulk-liquidation sales amid weak demand.
