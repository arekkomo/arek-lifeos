# Cash Flow Matrix — Metro Vancouver (July 2026 Pricing)

## Current Rate Environment (Jul 2026)
| Instrument | Rate | Status |
|-----------|------|--------|

## Key Observations
1. Positive cash-flow on a new purchase investment condo in Metro Vancouver does not currently exist at any price point unless you're getting creative with multiple tenants or short-term rentals, which carry their own legal risks
2. The $600K tier gets closest to break-even because prices in that budget segment have fallen more sharply and quickly than rents did — yields actually improved ~two percentage points at market bottom since early 2025
3. Optimal mortgage amount for positive cash flow: **$390,000 max** (210K down payment on sub-$600K unit). Anything above that mortgage size becomes negative cash-flow territory
