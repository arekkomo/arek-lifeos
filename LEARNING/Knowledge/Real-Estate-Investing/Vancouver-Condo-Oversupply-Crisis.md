# Vancouver Condo Oversupply Crisis (as of Jul 2026)

## Situation
Metro Vancouver has record-high unabsorbed new condo inventory. CMHC confirmed this in June 2026 that levels are all-time highs across the Metro region. REBGV data shows sales remain approximately 32% below normal absorption rates despite minor late-spring volume upticks — meaning distressed sellers outnumber healthy buyers.

## Supply Pipeline
New developments keep getting completed on schedules locked in three years ago when forecasts looked completely different. Thousands of brand-new condos sit empty because nobody can afford them and existing owners wouldn't take a loss to sell theirs (prices still searching for floor as TD Economics stated June 2026).

## Geographic Hotspots
- Brentwood, Metrotown, Surrey — oversupply zones projected worse than original forecasts
- Downtown core — prices back to pre-G20 (2009) levels at some sales

## Demand Destruction
- Triple-digit rates pulled buyers out of bidding wars entirely starting April 2026 mortgage renewal cliff peak
- Stricter affordability rules permanently removed roughly 10% of market demand base
- Government $3.2B condo buyout program (announced Jun 25, 2026) confirms that private demand cannot absorb current supply in normal timeline
