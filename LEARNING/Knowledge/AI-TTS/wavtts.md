# WavTTS

**By:** ByteDance
**Released:** 2026
**GitHub:** WavTTS-ComfyUI extension: https://github.com/Saganaki22/WavTTS-ComfyUI
**Project Page:** https://wavtts.github.io/

---

## What It Is

High-quality zero-shot text-to-speech generator. Creates natural-sounding speech in any voice from a short reference audio clip — no per-voice training required.

---

## Key Architecture

- **Zero-shot**: No per-voice training needed — clones any voice from a short reference
- **High-fidelity synthesis**: Produces natural, expressive speech
- **ComfyUI integration**: Available as ComfyUI nodes for workflow integration
- **Dynamic VRAM**: Optimized for efficient memory usage during inference

---

## Capabilities

- Zero-shot voice cloning from short reference audio
- High-quality text-to-speech synthesis
- ComfyUI node integration
- Dynamic VRAM management for efficient inference
- Multi-purpose voice generation

---

## VFX / Filmmaking Applications

- **ADR replacement**: Generate clean synthetic dialogue to replace problematic on-set audio
- **Voice synthesis for animation**: Create voice tracks for animated/CGI characters
- **Voiceover generation**: Produce voiceover content from text scripts
- **Dubbing/sync**: Create localized audio for international distribution
- **Audio post-production**: Generate specific audio content without voice actor scheduling
