# MiniMax

AI video generation platform. Stands out for text-to-video scene generation suited to full AI film production pipelines.

## Use in production

Positioned as the video scene generation layer in automated AI film workflows:
- Script/prompt → MiniMax (video scene) → DaVinci Resolve 19 (compilation)
- Paired with ChatGPT for prompt writing and Canva for thumbnails/design

## H3 update

[[MiniMax H3 Official Source Summary]] documents the current H3 model: text,
first/last-frame image, and multimodal reference-to-audio-video generation with
native stereo audio. Use [[MiniMax H3 Prompting Guide]] for T2V/I2V prompts and
[[MiniMax H3 Reference and Audio Workflow]] for R2V with labelled references.
