# SkyReels-A2

Skywork AI's audio-to-video model for generating talking-head animations. Takes an **input audio file** and produces a character that performs accurate lip-syncing while also animating body movement and background — going beyond pure mouth animation to full-body performance.

## Key Features
- Audio-driven character animation (lip-sync + body)
- Background generation conditioned on audio mood/context
- Support for custom character avatars
- Natural head poses, gestures, and micro-expressions aligned to speech

## Use Cases
Creating narrated video content from text/voiceovers, AI news anchors, character dialogue scenes in AI filmmaking pipelines.

> ⚠️ Cross-domain: `[[X-Portrait2]]` offers similar face-only control; SkyReels-A2 goes further with full-body motion. For emotion mapping from voice, see audio domain entry on `[[MMAudio]]`.

## Installation
Access via [Skywork AI GitHub](https://github.com/SkyworkAI/SkyReels-A2). Requires GPU for inference (model available on HuggingFace).
