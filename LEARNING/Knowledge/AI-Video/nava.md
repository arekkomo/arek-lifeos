## What It Is

Video generation framework with natively integrated synchronized audio. Unlike previous approaches where audio was added post-generation, NAVA generates video and audio simultaneously for perfect sync — critical for realistic dialogue-driven content.

---

## Key Architecture

- **Native audio-visual generation**: Audio and video generated as a unified output, not post-added
- **Temporal synchronization**: Built-in sync between audio and visual frames
- **ERNIE model**: Leverages Baidu's ERNIE foundation models
- **End-to-end pipeline**: Generates both audio and video in one pass

---

## Capabilities

- Simultaneous video and audio generation
- Natively synchronized audio-visual output
- High-fidelity synced speech and gestures
- Context-aware audio generation matching video content

---

## VFX / Filmmaking Applications

- **Dialogue-heavy short films**: Generate realistic talking-head scenes with synced lip movement
- **Character animation**: Create animated characters with consistent voice and movement
- **Automated dubbing**: Generate localized versions with synced lip motion
- **VR/AR character content**: Create realistic talking characters for VR/AR experiences
- **Automated pre-vis**: Generate pre-vis scenes with synchronized dialogue and action
- **Educational content**: Create animated educational videos with natural voice sync
