# DomoAI

Video-to-AI-animation tool. Takes existing live-action video and transforms it into various AI animation styles. Highlighted as a top tool in its category by creator Jon Draper.

## Capability

- Input: live video clip
- Output: stylized AI animation (various art styles)
- Integrates with other AI tools for more advanced animation projects

## Compared to

- [[runway-ml]] — Runway focuses more on text-to-video and video editing; DomoAI specializes in stylization
- [[ai-animation]] — DomoAI is a key tool in the AI animation workflow
