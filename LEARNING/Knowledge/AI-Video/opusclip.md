# OpusClip

Automated AI video clipping tool. Ingests long-form video and outputs short-form clips optimized for virality and platform sharing.

## Use case

- Long-form video (YouTube, podcast, interview) → auto-clipped shorts
- Removes the manual labor of identifying and cutting best moments
- Relevant for content creators repurposing content across platforms
