## Description from Notion

> OpusClip is positioned as '#1 AI video clipping tool to create viral shorts' — an automated platform for extracting and refining short-form content from longer source footage using AI-powered scene detection and editing optimization. This represents the 'clip and automate' end of the production pipeline, where raw footage gets rapidly reduced into platform-optimized formats without manual timeline editing in DaVinci Resolve or similar DAWs.

## Key Capabilities
- **Scene detection:** Automatic identification of highlight moments for clipping
- **Caption generation:** Subtitle overlay with platform-specific formatting (TikTok/Reels/Shorts optimized)
- **Aspect ratio transformation:** 16:9 source → 9:16 vertical conversion with subject tracking
- **Platform optimization:** Content specifically refined for maximum engagement on short-form platforms
