## Description
ReCamMaster re-captures existing videos with new camera movements. It uses pre-trained text-to-video models and a video conditioning mechanism to reproduce the dynamic scene of an input video from novel camera trajectories. This tool can also be used for video stabilization, data augmentation, and autonomous driving.
