# Runway ML

One of the leading AI video platforms. Used extensively for text-to-video, image-to-video, and AI-powered video editing. Frequently paired with other tools (Kling AI, DaVinci Resolve) in production pipelines.

## Common pairings

- **Runway + Kling AI** — for 3D animation creation workflows
- **Runway + DaVinci Resolve** — generation then professional post-production
- Adobe referenced Runway-style generative AI tools in Premiere Pro AI workflows

## Key capabilities

- Text-to-video generation
- Image-to-video (animate a still)
- Video-to-video style transfer
- AI-powered video editing tools
