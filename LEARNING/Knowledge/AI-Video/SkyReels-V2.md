# SkyReels-V2

Skywork AI's second-generation video generation model. Produces cinematic-quality text-to-video and image-to-video outputs — positioned as a competitor to Wan, Kling, and Hunyuan in the open-source T2V space.

## Key Features
- Text-conditioned and image-conditioned video generation
- Cinematic aesthetic tuning (compared to purely realistic models)
- Open weights available for self-hosting
- Supports aspect ratios used in filmmaking (2.39:1, 16:9, etc.)

## Use Cases
Generating stylized cinematic clips for pre-vis, mood films, and AI-assisted storyboarding where aesthetic quality matters more than photorealism.

> ⚠️ Compare: `[[Wan-Alpha]]` offers RGBA compositable output; SkyReels focuses on pure generation quality.

## Installation
Access weights via [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/SkyworkAI) or follow setup in the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/SkyworkAI/SkyReels-V2).
