# FilmPort

FilmPort describes itself as an "operating system" for professional-grade AI video production, targeting end-to-end creative production workflows for professionals.

## What it does

- Provides a unified platform for AI-assisted film production
- Covers the full pipeline from ideation through production
- Positioned for professional users rather than hobbyists

## Significance

Most AI video tools (Runway, MiniMax, Kling) focus on individual generation tasks. FilmPort positions as a production coordination layer — analogous to a DAW or NLE but for AI video workflows.
