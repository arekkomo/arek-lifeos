# AI Avatar & Lipsync

The convergence of digital human creation, voice synthesis, and motion capture through AI.

## Digital doubles

**[[cap4d|CAP4D]]** — creates professional-grade portrait avatars for VFX/filmmaking from stills or short video. Democratizes what previously required expensive photogrammetry rigs.

## Motion capture

**[[move-ai|Move AI]]** — markerless, suitless motion capture. Uses AI + computer vision + physics to capture human motion including finger detail. No studio required.

## Dubbing & lipsync

**just-dub-it** — full dubbing pipeline: ASR → translation → lipsync. Multilingual video content from a single shoot.

## Voice synthesis & cloning

**chatterbox** — real-time streaming voice output with voice cloning support. Built for interactive AI applications. Self-hostable.

## Production implications

These tools together make it possible to:
1. Capture performance cheaply (Move AI for mocap, iPhone for digital double source)
2. Place that performance in any visual context (CAP4D digital double)
3. Dub the result into any language (just-dub-it)
4. Synthesize custom voice (chatterbox)

→ Full synthetic human performance pipeline accessible to indie filmmakers.
