# [[Reve 2]] (Source)

> ⚠️ Synthesis opportunity: Reve 2's multi-speaker cloning + emotion control maps directly to directorial workflow. For a film project, you could clone actor voices from short reference clips and generate multiple performance takes with emotional variations — all AI-generated — before committing real actors.

## Summary
Reve 2 is Meta's latest voice synthesis engine offering ultra-realistic, emotionally nuanced text-to-speech output. Designed as a foundation model for speech generation, it supports speaker-agnostic generation (talking in any voice you specify), speaker cloning from short audio references, and controllable emotional expressiveness — all at production-grade quality suitable for professional content pipelines.
