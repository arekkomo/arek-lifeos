# NVIDIA Edify

Enterprise-grade AI image and 3D content generation system from NVIDIA. Targets professional 3D production workflows with topology and texture quality levels suitable for film, games, and industrial applications.

## Why it matters

Most AI 3D generation tools (Tripo AI, 3D AI Studio) prioritize speed and ease for rough assets. Edify prioritizes **production-ready quality**:
- **Clean quads-based topology** — not the messy triangulated meshes typical of AI-generated 3D
- **4K PBR textures** — physically based rendering support for professional pipelines
- **AI Foundry integration** — enterprises can customize the model for their specific asset styles

## Key capabilities

- Enterprise AI image generation
- AI 3D model generation with professional topology
- 4K PBR texture generation
- Customization via NVIDIA AI Foundry (fine-tuning for domain-specific output)
