# CAP4D

AI tool for generating digital doubles — personalized, professional-grade portrait avatars for use in VFX and filmmaking. Input is minimal: stills or a short video of the subject.

## Significance

Digital doubles previously required:
- Photogrammetry rigs with dozens of cameras
- Hours of scanning and manual cleanup
- Expensive VFX studio pipelines

CAP4D dramatically lowers the barrier — consumer-grade input, professional-grade output.

## Use cases

- VFX: create a digital double for a stunt or scene
- Filmmaking: de-aging, resurrection effects, CG character work
- Virtual production: personalized digital actors
