# dejavu

AI tool/research platform that accelerates photogrammetry, simulation, and 3D reconstruction workflows across VFX, real estate, gaming, and industrial applications.

## What it does

- Applies AI to tasks that previously required expensive manual optimization:
  - Photogrammetry reconstruction
  - Simulation (physics, materials)
  - 3D scene generation
- Targets VFX, gaming, real estate, and simulation industries

## Significance

Photogrammetry and simulation pipelines are computationally and manually intensive. dejavu applies AI acceleration to reduce both cost and time, democratizing access to high-quality 3D reconstruction.
