# Volinga

Professional desktop suite for [[gaussian-splatting|Gaussian Splatting]] and radiance field workflows, targeting VFX, television, and entertainment production.

## What it does

- Captures and reconstructs high-quality 3D scenes from real-world footage using radiance field techniques (Gaussian Splatting)
- Desktop application designed for professional production use, not just research
- Outputs usable in VFX pipelines and virtual production

## Significance

Gaussian Splatting enables real-time, high-fidelity 3D scene representation. Volinga wraps the underlying research into a professional tool pipeline, making it viable for episodic TV and film VFX without deep technical expertise.

## Positioning

- Targets professionals who would otherwise use photogrammetry rigs or LIDAR scanning
- Competes/complements: [[dejavu]] (photogrammetry + AI), [[lyra]] (iPhone-to-3D)
- Positioned for broadcast/episodic rather than indie filmmaking

## Use cases

- VFX: 3D scan of locations for set extensions
- Virtual production: real-world environment reconstruction for LED volume stages
- Television: fast turnaround 3D assets from footage
