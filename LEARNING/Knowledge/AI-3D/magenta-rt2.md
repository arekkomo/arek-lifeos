# Magenta RealTime 2

**By:** Google
**Released:** 2026
**GitHub:** https://github.com/KytraScript/magenta-rt2 (Local runner + web GUI for Apple Silicon)
**Project Page:** https://magenta.withgoogle.com/magenta-realtime-2

---

## What It Is

Google's open-source low-latency realtime music generator. Can generate music in real-time, suitable for live performance integration, interactive soundtracks, and spontaneous musical composition.

---

## Key Architecture

- **Realtime generation**: Low-latency music synthesis for live interaction
- **Google Magenta**: Built on Google's Magenta research framework
- **Apple Silicon support**: MLX backend available via KytraScript runner
- **Local inference**: Can run locally on consumer hardware

---

## Capabilities

- Realtime music generation with low latency
- Interactive/generative music capabilities
- Apple Silicon (MLX) support
- Open source under Google Magenta project

---

## VFX / Filmmaking Applications

- **Live score generation**: Generate music scores in real-time during film editing
- **Interactive soundtracks**: Create games/VR with dynamic generative score
- **Mood-matching**: Generate background music that matches scene mood
- **Rapid prototype scoring**: Generate rough score ideas before full composition
- **Performance art**: Live musical accompaniment for visual performances
