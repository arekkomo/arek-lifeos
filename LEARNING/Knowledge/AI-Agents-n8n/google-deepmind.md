# Google DeepMind

Alphabet's AI research organization, formed by merging Google Brain and DeepMind. Primary output relevant to this wiki: the **Gemini** model family.

## Gemini 2.0

Released late 2024. Key framing: **"new AI model for the agentic era"** — the first major model explicitly designed around agentic use cases rather than chat/assistant use.

Capabilities:
- Multimodal: language, image generation, video generation
- Designed for tool use and multi-step reasoning
- Signals frontier labs treating agents as primary use case, not afterthought

## Significance for agentic workflows

Gemini 2.0's explicit agentic framing means:
- Better tool-use reliability out of the box
- Multimodal inputs/outputs useful for creative pipelines
- Competitor benchmark for Claude in agentic contexts
