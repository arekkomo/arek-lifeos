# Suno Music Style Tags Guide

> **RULE ZERO: Never use artist or band names in any Suno field.** This guide uses only sonic descriptors. A track should be identifiable by its instrumentation, tempo, and emotional character — never by who "made it famous."

## How to Use This Guide

Build style prompts by selecting **one tag from each category** that matches your intended sound. Combine across categories with commas or brief descriptive phrases. v5 supports ~1,000 characters in the Style field — use them wisely.

---

## 1. BPM / Tempo Descriptor Tags

Suno responds to tempo descriptions as well as numerical BPM values. Using both is most effective.

### Ultra-Slow (40-65 BPM)
- **Tags:** `downtempo`, `slowcore`, `dirge-like`, `plodding`, ` Funeral march tempo`
- **Best for:** Drone, ambient, dark ambient, sludge/doom metal
- **Prompt examples:** "ultra-slow plodding at 50 BPM", "funeral-march tempo dragging through darkness"

### Slow (65-90 BPM)
- **Tags:** `ballad tempo`, `slow jam`, `half-time groove`
- **Best for:** Ballads, R&B ballads, slow blues, trip-hop, lofi
- **Prompt examples:** "slow ballad at 72 BPM", "half-time hip-hop groove at 80 BPM"

### Mid-Slow (90-110 BPM)
- **Tags:** `moderate pace`, `relaxed tempo`, `mid-tempo`
- **Best for:** Reggae, chill hop, singer-songwriter, downtempo electronic
- **Prompt examples:** "relaxed reggae tempo at 95 BPM", "mid-tempo fingerpicked acoustic"

### Mid (110-128 BPM) — **Most common pop/rock sweet spot**
- **Tags:** `standard tempo`, `driving mid-tempo`, `upbeat moderate`
- **Best for:** Pop, rock, alternative, electronic dance (lower range), indie, country
- **Prompt examples:** "driving 120 BPM pop rhythm", "standard tempo with steady kick drum"

### Mid-Fast (128-150 BPM)
- **Tags:** `upbeat`, `energetic pace`, `fast groove`, `double-time feel`
- **Best for:** Drum and bass, upbeat pop-punk, disco, house, eurodance
- **Prompt examples:** "driving 140 BPM drum and bass rhythm", "fast double-time guitar riff"

### Fast (150-180 BPM)
- **Tags:** `frantic pace`, `blasting`, `breakneck tempo`, `hyperactive`
- **Best for:** Hardcore punk, speed metal, hardcore electronic, gabber
- **Prompt examples:** "blasting 170 BPM punk blast", "frantic breakneck guitar work"

### Ultra-Fast (180+ BPM)
- **Tags:** `machine-gun pace`, `insane tempo`
- **Best for:** UK hardcore, powerviolence, extreme speedcore
- **Prompt examples:** "insane 200 BPM blast beats and wall of distortion"

## 3. Production Quality & Texture Tags

These describe HOW the recording is made/sounded, not what instruments are used.

| Tag / Description | Characteristic sound | Genre associations |
|------------------|---------------------|--------------------|
| `lofi cassette tape warmth` | Saturated, compressed, slightly warbled | Lo-fi hip-hop, indie bedroom |
| `crisp modern digital production` | Clean wide dynamic range, surgical clarity | Modern pop, EDM, hyperpop |
| `vintage analog warmth` | Tube/saturation coloration, slight compression | Classic rock, soul, jazz |
| `lo-fi bedroom recording aesthetic` | Intimate close-mic'd, slightly raw edges | Indie folk, bedroom pop, lofi |
| `room-tight studio sound` | Close-proximity vocal with room reverb bleed | 60s pop, garage rock |
| `ambient reverb-drenched` | Massive hall/plate reverb on all instruments | Shoegaze, dream pop, ambient |
| `dry intimate close-mic vocals` | Vocal sits right in listener's ear, minimal FX | Singer-songwriter, rap, lo-fi |
| `lo-fi vinyl crackle background` | Record surface noise + slight warp/wow | Retrowave, nostalgic feel |
| `harsh lo-fi distortion` | Intentional clipping, bit-crushing, degradation | Noise, grindcore, DIY punk |
| `spatial stereo imaging` | Wide left-right panning, immersive soundfield | Electronic, ambient, 360 sound design |
| `mono phase-restricted vocal` | Centered narrow focal point for voice | Retro pop, lo-fi, experimental |

## 4. Vocal Character Descriptors

> **Do NOT use artist names to describe voices.** Instead, build a person's voice from these observable qualities.

### Voice Quality
| Descriptor | Sound characterised | Example prompt fragment |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| `smoky husky alto` | Warm lowered tone with grain texture | "A smoky husky vocal..." |
| `clear bright soprano` | High, ringing, transparent quality | "...with clear bright soprano delivery" |
| `raspy rasping voice` | Gritty, friction-heavy vocal texture | "...raspy voice pushing through grit" |
| `breathy whispery delivery` | Air-forwarded, intimate sound | "...breathy whispery vocal style" |
| `powerful chest-voice belt` | Strong thick chest register projection | "...powerful chest belting across octaves" |
| `head-voice falsetto lightness` | Light airy upper-register voice | "...light ethereal head-voice falsetto" |
| `deep gravelly baritone` | Low rumbling, textured male voice | "...deep gravelly baritone with gravel" |
| `androgynous gender-neutral delivery` | Voice blurs traditional gender markers | Androgynous voice works for genre-fluid work |

### Vocal Delivery / Technique
- `` [Whispered] `` — Soft spoken-singing
- `` [Falsetto] `` — Light head register
- `` [Belted] `` — Strong chest projection
- `` [Raspy] ```/`` [Gritty] `` — Textured distortion
- ```[Breathy] ``` — Air-heavy intimate delivery
- `` [Spoken Word] `` — Rhythmic speech
- `` [Melismatic] `` — Multiple notes per syllable
- `` [Staccato] `` - Detached short syllables
- `` [Legato] ```/`` Smooth connected phrasing
- `` [Vibrato] ```/`` Slight pitch oscillation on sustained notes

### Vocal Persona Examples (use these instead of "sounds like...")
```
"A weathered blues singer with a gravelly tenor who cracks on the high notes"
"A young indie vocalist with a breathy upper register and slight tremble in the verses"
"A seasoned soul crooner with rich baritone warmth and controlled vibrato"
"A punk rock lead voice — raw unpolished, slightly off-melody but full of conviction"
```

## 6. Genre Tags (Combinatorial)

Combining contrasting genres produces the best results in Suno:

### Foundational Genre Tags
```
pop / rock / hip-hop / electronic / R&B / soul / jazz
country / folk / blues / classical crossover / punk / metal / reggae
lofi / house / techno / ambient / dubstep / synthwave / darkwave
gospel / choir / new age / world music / flamenco / bossa nova
```

### Hybrid Genre Combos (high effectiveness):
- `bossa nova electronic` — warm grooves with digital textures
- `Appalachian gothic` — haunting mountain harmonies with dark atmosphere
- `chiptune jazz` — game-music arpeggios through jazz chord changes
- `drum and bass folk` — rapid breakbeats under acoustic melodies
- **synthwave noir** - retro synth beds with detective film tension
- `punk reggae fusion` - snarling chords over bounce rhythms
- `industrial classical crossover` — mechanical percussion meets orchestral strings

## Building a Complete Style Prompt — Example

Template:
```
[Genre + BPM] | [Mood] | [Instruments - primary, secondary, rhythm section] | [Vocal persona] | [Production quality] | [Dynamics arc across song]
```

Full example (~900 chars for v5):
```
Mid-tempo synthwave noir at 112 BPM. Haunting detective film score with swelling strings, pulsing Moog bass arpeggios, and tight snare backbeat. A smoky husky male vocal delivers weathered narration-style verses that build to powerful belted chorus. Vintage analog warmth production with wide stereo imaging. Starts intimate close-mic whisper, gradually layers electronics, explosive final chorus with orchestrals swell, collapses into sparse piano outro fading to silence.
```

**Tagged breakdown:**
| Component | Value in prompt |
|-----------|----------------|
| Genre+BPM | `Mid-tempo synthwave noir at 112 BPM` |
| Mood | `Haunting detective film score` |
| Primary instrument | `swelling strings section, pulsing Moog bass arpeggios` |
| Secondary instrument | `tight snare drum backbeat` |
| Vocal persona | `smoky husky male vocal delivers weathered narration-style verses that build to powerful belted chorus` |
| Production quality | `Vintage analog warmth production with wide stereo imaging` |
| Dynamics arc | `Starts intimate close-mic whisper, gradually layers electronics, explosive final chorus with orchestrals swell, collapses into sparse piano outro fading to silence` |
