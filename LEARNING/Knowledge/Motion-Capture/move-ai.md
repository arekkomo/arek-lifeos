# Move AI

AI-powered motion capture that requires no suit, no markers, and no specialized studio. Uses advanced AI, computer vision, and physics models to capture high-quality human motion including finger detail.

## Why it matters

Traditional motion capture requires:
- Expensive suits with reflective markers
- Controlled studio environments
- Dedicated hardware

Move AI removes all of these requirements, making professional-quality mocap accessible to independent filmmakers and animators.

## Accuracy

Captures finger-level detail — historically the hardest part of markerless mocap.
