# Notion Export — AI Video & Animation

Personal curation from the dtb Knowledge Notion database. 15 entries spanning tutorials, tools, research papers, and platforms in AI-driven video and animation production.

## Coverage

**Production pipelines**
- Full AI film production: MiniMax (scene gen) + ChatGPT (scripting) + Canva (design) + DaVinci Resolve 19 (compilation)
- Runway ML + Kling AI paired for 3D animation creation
- OpusClip for automated short-form clip generation from long-form content

**Video-to-video & stylization**
- [[domoai|DomoAI]] — live video to AI-animated stylization
- Stable Diffusion integrated at hardware level: [[cmr-m1|CMR M1 AI Cinema Camera]]
- Lucy-Edit-ComfyUI — instruction-guided video editing in ComfyUI

**Research & open-source**
- [[open-sora|Open-Sora]] — open-source Sora alternative (text-to-video + image-to-video)
- Light-A-Video — training-free temporally consistent lighting control
- [[enhance-a-video|Enhance-A-Video]] — quality enhancement layer on existing models (Feb 2025)
- ReVideo — localized video editing with motion + content control simultaneously

**Avatar, dubbing & motion**
- [[cap4d|CAP4D]] — portrait digital doubles for VFX/filmmaking from stills or short video
- [[move-ai|Move AI]] — markerless, suitless motion capture including fingers
- just-dub-it — ASR → translation → lipsync dubbing pipeline
- chatterbox — real-time voice cloning and streaming TTS

**Agentic creative pipelines**
- [[agentic-creative-pipelines|Agentic creative pipelines]] outperform n8n/Weave for complex creative generation: agents trained on prompting best practices, more robust than node-based flows

## Key takeaways

1. The AI video tool landscape bifurcates: **text-to-video platforms** (MiniMax, Open-Sora, Runway) vs **video-to-video stylization** (DomoAI, Stable Diffusion-based tools)
2. **Hardware AI** is emerging as a category (CMR M1) — AI not just in software post but in-camera
3. **Digital humans are democratizing**: CAP4D makes professional digital doubles accessible from consumer-grade input
4. **Motion capture no longer requires suits** — Move AI achieves finger-level capture with computer vision alone
5. **Agentic pipelines** are the next evolution beyond manual n8n/automation for creative workflows
