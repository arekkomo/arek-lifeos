# Stability AI

Creator of the Stable Diffusion family and a broad open-source generative AI ecosystem spanning image, video, audio, and 3D. Notable for open-weight model releases.

## Key products

- **Stable Diffusion** — foundational image generation model; powers much of the open-source AI image ecosystem (ComfyUI, kohya_ss, Automatic1111)
- **Stable Audio Open** — open-source audio generation
- **Stable Artisan / Stable Assistant** — Discord-based media generation interface covering image, video, audio, and 3D generation; accessible entry point into Stability AI models without local setup
- **Edify** — enterprise image and 3D generation with customization via AI Foundry

## Legal context: Getty Images lawsuit

Stability AI is involved in active copyright litigation with Getty Images over Stable Diffusion's training data. This is a landmark case for the AI image generation industry — outcome will shape IP rights, licensing requirements, and which "ethical AI image generators" (artist-collaboration models) gain traction.
- Context: [[notion-export-ai-image-midjourney]] — "Artists Are Collaborating With a New 'Ethical' AI Image Generator" entry

## Partnership: OTOY + Endeavor + The Render Network (2024–2025)

Strategic partnership covering:
- **Decentralized GPU computing** via The Render Network for training and inference
- **IP rights systems** for AI-generated content — infrastructure for licensing and attribution
- Models optimized for distributed rendering at scale

Significance: signals that AI generation at scale requires new infrastructure and legal layers, not just better models.
