# Creative Flow Through Constraints

The insight: creative block is usually not a lack of ideas but **too many**. Infinite possibility triggers perfectionist paralysis. A system — fixed time, fixed constraint, concrete output goal — eliminates the meta-question and activates creative problem-solving.

---

## The Mechanism

**Open state:** "What should I create today?" → infinite options → nothing feels good enough → paralysis

**Constrained state:** "I'm writing 6 pages before 5pm" → brain switches to execution mode → flow

The constraint doesn't limit creativity. It *defines the playing field* so creative energy can go into the work rather than the selection process.

---

## Real Examples

- Stephen King: 6 pages/day, 365 days/year. "Amateurs wait for inspiration. The rest of us just get up and go to work."
- Paul McCartney: melody arrived in a dream; lyrics took months of daily work.
- David Lynch: same diner, same time every day, napkin notes — even the most "inspiration-driven" creators have scheduled daily practice.

---

## How to Apply It

1. **Fixed session time** — pre-decided, not chosen each day (e.g., 8pm creative window)
2. **Concrete output constraint** — "1 scene / 1 verse / 5 shot descriptions" not "make something good"
3. **Close with a capture** — one line: what did I make? (feeds journaling habit)
