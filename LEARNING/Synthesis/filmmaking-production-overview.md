# Filmmaking & Production — Overview

This synthesis covers the craft and technical side of filmmaking and content creation.

## Topic clusters

- **Storytelling & Narrative** — structure, character, genre, scriptwriting
- **Content Creation** — formats, platforms, audience, attention engineering
- **VFX** — compositing, motion graphics, 3D reconstruction, digital doubles
- **Film Editing** — pacing, rhythm, transitions, narrative editing theory
- **[[davinci-resolve|DaVinci Resolve]]** — color grading, Fusion, editing workflows, shortcuts, plugins
- **[[cinematic-shooting|Cinematic Shooting]]** — the cinematic triangle, picture profiles, shooting-to-grade pipeline

## Key questions this wiki tracks

- What are the core storytelling principles that hold across formats (short, long, social)?
- What are the best DaVinci Resolve workflows for speed and quality?
- How do VFX techniques integrate with AI-generated content?
- What editing patterns make content more engaging?

## AI entering the filmmaking pipeline (2026)

### 3D scene capture & reconstruction
- [[volinga|Volinga]] — professional Gaussian Splatting desktop suite for TV/film/entertainment
- [[lyra|Lyra]] — iPhone video → 3D environment reconstruction for VFX/virtual production
- [[dejavu|dejavu]] — AI-accelerated photogrammetry and simulation for VFX, gaming, real estate
- See [[gaussian-splatting]] for the underlying technology

### Digital doubles & performance
- [[cap4d|CAP4D]] — professional digital doubles from stills/short video; no photogrammetry rig needed
- [[move-ai|Move AI]] — markerless mocap including fingers; no suit, no studio

### Production OS & workflow platforms
- [[filmport|FilmPort]] — "operating system" for professional AI video production; end-to-end pipeline

### Video generation as production input
- [[minimax|MiniMax]], [[runway-ml|Runway ML]], [[kling-ai|Kling AI]] — generate base footage or scene elements
- [[domoai|DomoAI]] — stylize live footage into animation

### Hardware AI
- CMR M1 AI Cinema Camera — Stable Diffusion built into camera hardware; video-to-video processing in-camera

### Post-production AI
- [[opusclip|OpusClip]] — automated short-form clip generation
- just-dub-it — multilingual dubbing pipeline
- Adobe Premiere Pro — generative AI tools for workflow acceleration
