## Summary

SoulX-Singer is a practical singing voice synthesis and conversion framework that enables zero-shot generation of realistic singing voices from text, MIDI, or audio inputs, with strong control over pitch, rhythm, and timbre.

## Features

- Zero-shot singing voice synthesis
- MIDI and F0-conditioned generation
- Singing voice conversion (SVC)
- Cross-lingual support (EN, CN, Cantonese)
- Timbre cloning and style transfer
- Lyrics editing with expression preservation
- WebUI and CLI support
- Pretrained models available

## Use Cases

- AI music production and virtual singers
- AI covers and remixing
- Voice/style transfer for singers
- Multilingual singing synthesis
- Research in generative music AI

## Installation

Clone repo, install dependencies, download pretrained weights from provided links, and run WebUI or CLI inference scripts.
