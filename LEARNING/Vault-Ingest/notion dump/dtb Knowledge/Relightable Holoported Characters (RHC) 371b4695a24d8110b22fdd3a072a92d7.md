## About

RHC captures full-body human performance from 4 sparse RGB cameras and relights it under arbitrary environment maps in a single neural network pass. No OLAT-basis capture required. CVPR 2026 Oral.

**Project:** [https://vcai.mpi-inf.mpg.de/projects/RHC/](https://vcai.mpi-inf.mpg.de/projects/RHC/)

## Capabilities

- Full-body human relighting from 4 sparse RGB cameras
- Single-pass transformer RelightNet; arbitrary environment map lighting
- Free-viewpoint rendering of relit humans
- Telepresence: multiple avatars consistently relit to shared digital environment

## VFX / Filmmaking Use Cases

- Place a real actor under entirely different lighting without reshooting
- Telepresence production: actors in different locations composited with consistent lighting
- Aiah Syn live capture: relight performers to match virtual stage
