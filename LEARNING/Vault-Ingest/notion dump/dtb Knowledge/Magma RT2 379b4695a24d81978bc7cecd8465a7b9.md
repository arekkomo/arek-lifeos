## About

Open-source low-latency realtime music generation engine by Google Magenta Team. Transformer-based framework for instrument and voice synthesis with real-time inference.

## Capabilities

- Low-latency realtime music generation from prompts
- Transformer-based framework for instrument and voice synthesis
- Real-time inference capabilities for interactive applications
- Open-source support from Google's Magenta research team

---

## VFX / Filmmaking Use Cases

- Generate realtime soundtracks for interactive content
- Create dynamic audio that responds to content generation
- Prototype musical direction for scene composition
- Generate variations of ambient soundtracks instantaneously
