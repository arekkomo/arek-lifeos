## AI PHOTOBOOTH KIOSK

PRODUCTION:

T&DA

CLIENT:

The Lume

AGENCY:

Grande

[T&DA](https://www.linkedin.com/company/19048697/admin/feed/posts/#) created an AI generated Photo Booth for The Lume Melbourne. 

We developed a realtime Python solution, that used a ComfyUI backend.

With the use of IP-adapters and ControlNets, we were able to translate the participants full body photo into a historical context by directly using DaVinci’s Codex Leicester as the style input.

For the production side, we created a historically accurate and authentic scene bringing DaVinci back to virtual life, at our [Steelbridge Studio](https://www.linkedin.com/company/19048697/admin/feed/posts/#).  

Manufacturing a state of the art kiosk that will stand the test of time, our final install is complete and ready for the large volumn of interaction it is already recieving.

As he beckons visitors to Melbourne’s The Lume Leonardo da Vinci – 500 Years Of Genius experience, to have their likeness drawn, visitors get to take home with them, or share on social a unique AI drawing, innovation as the great man himself would have enjoyed.
