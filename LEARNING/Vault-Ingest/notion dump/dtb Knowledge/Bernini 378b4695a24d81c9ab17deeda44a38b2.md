# Bernini

Tags: AI Video, VFX, Video Generation
Description: Unified framework for video generation and editing that combines an MLLM-based semantic planner with a DiT-based renderer. Reaches first tier among commercial models for video synthesis. Supports text-to-video, image-to-video, and video editing with precise content, style, and structure control.
