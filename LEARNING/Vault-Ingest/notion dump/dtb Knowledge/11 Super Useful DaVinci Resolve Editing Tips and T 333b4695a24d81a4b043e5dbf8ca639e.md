## 💡 Key Insights

- Magic Mask version two can replace expensive VFX work for tasks like recoloring specific objects in footage
- The auto remove silences feature can save hours of manual editing time on talking head content
- Custom ruler guides finally bring Photoshop-like precision alignment to DaVinci Resolve's viewer

## 📋 All Points & Tips

- Custom ruler guides can be dragged from rulers onto the viewer to create precise alignment guides, similar to Photoshop
- Guide colors can be changed by right-clicking and selecting guide color, and guides can be removed or hidden without deleting them
- The auto remove silences feature under Clip > Audio Operations > Ripple Delete Silence automatically cuts out silent sections from talking head clips
- You can adjust the volume threshold slider to control what gets detected as silence before removing it
- Adding automatic crossfades to silence cuts makes the audio flow more naturally between edits
- Magic Mask version two can be used in the color page to select and recolor specific objects like shoe stripes without VFX software
- Hold Alt or Option while clicking to subtract areas from your Magic Mask selection
- Use the two arrows in Magic Mask to track the mask forward and backward across the entire clip
- Magic Mask can be used in the Fusion page to isolate a subject and place graphics behind them
- Duplicate your video clip to two tracks, apply Magic Mask to the top clip, then insert graphics between the layers
- Add drop shadows to the upper clip to improve the layered graphic effect
- Push animations can be added to graphics that appear behind masked subjects
- Shift plus Spacebar opens the tools menu in the Fusion page to quickly find and add effects
- The color page allows you to hide layers to simplify your grading workflow when dealing with adjustment clips and B-roll
