## Summary

HiStream is an autoregressive diffusion-based framework for high-resolution video generation. It removes redundant computation across spatial, temporal, and diffusion-step dimensions, enabling efficient streaming video synthesis with high fidelity and speed.

## Features

- Spatial compression for low-res caching and high-res refinement
- Temporal compression via chunked processing and anchor caches
- Timestep compression to minimize redundant denoising
- Up to 76x–107x faster than standard video diffusion methods
- Designed for scalable, high-resolution video synthesis

## Use Cases

- Accelerated AI video generation for research and production
- Efficient high-res video synthesis for creative workflows
- Diffusion model optimization for low-latency applications
