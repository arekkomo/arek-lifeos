## About

InstructAV2AV jointly edits audio and video from a single text instruction — change visual appearance, spoken words, speaker identity, and voice timbre in one unified pass.

**GitHub:** [https://github.com/suimuc/InstructAV2AV](https://github.com/suimuc/InstructAV2AV)

**Paper:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18467](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18467)

**Project:** [https://hjzheng.net/projects/InstructAV2AV/](https://hjzheng.net/projects/InstructAV2AV/)

## Capabilities

- Identity-preserving speech modification
- AV instance editing, insertion, and removal
- Appearance/content/timbre-preserving modes
- Outperforms AvED, CoherentAVEdit, AVI-Edit

## VFX / Filmmaking Use Cases

- Replace actor in a scene — appearance + voice sync in one pass
- Correct dialogue post-shoot: change spoken words while preserving identity
- Aiah Syn: swap visual identity while preserving vocal performance
