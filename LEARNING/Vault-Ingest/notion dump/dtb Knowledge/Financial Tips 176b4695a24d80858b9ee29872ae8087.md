**The 4 Key Numbers to Track** 

1. **Fixed Costs (50-60%):**
    - Rent/mortgage, groceries, debt payments.
2. **Savings (5-10%):**
    - Emergency funds, major purchase savings.
3. **Investments (5-10%):**
    - Wealth-building through consistent contributions.
4. **Guilt-Free Spending (20-35%):**
    - Money for things you love without guilt (e.g., travel, hobbies).

**10 Money Rules Mentioned in the Video** 

1. **Always Have One Year of Emergency Funds**
    - Keep this in a liquid savings account, not as physical cash, to handle unexpected situations.
2. **Save 10% and Invest 20% of Gross Annual Income**
    - Ensure financial security by aggressively investing a portion of your income.
3. **Pay Cash for Large Expenses**
    - Avoid debt for significant purchases like engagement rings, holidays, or weddings by saving beforehand.
4. **Never Question Spending on Books, Appetizers, Health, or Donating to a Friend's Charity Fundraiser**
    - Value personal growth, experiences, and giving back over hesitating on these expenses.
5. **Business Class Flights for Flights Over Four Hours Long**
    - Prioritize comfort and health for long-distance travel.
6. **Buy the Best and Keep It as Long as Possible**
    - Invest in quality items and use them extensively to maximize value.
7. **No Limit on Spending for Health or Education**
    - Allocate unlimited resources for personal well-being and knowledge acquisition.
8. **Invest Aggressively**
    - Regularly contribute to investments to secure long-term wealth.
9. **Keep Costs Low**
    - Limit fees in investments, as excessive charges significantly erode returns over time.
10. **Marry the Right Person**
- Recognize the financial and emotional impact of a supportive partnership on your long-term goals.
