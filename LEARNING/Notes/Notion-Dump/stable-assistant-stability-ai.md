- What is Stable Assistant?
    
    Stable Assistant is a friendly chatbot powered by Stability AI’s text and image generation technology, featuring *Stable Diffusion 3* and *Stable LM 2 12B*. It excels in generating images from conversational prompts, offering knowledgeable responses, helping with writing projects, and enhancing content with complimentary matching images. Capable of generating images in various styles, Stable Assistant leans towards an illustration style in some specific use cases.

- How does Stable Assistant pricing work?
    
    You will be deducted credits for each usage, for instance 6.5 credits for a successful image generation and 0.1 credit for each message sent to Stable Assistant. For exhaustive costs of all tools please refer the [Stable Assistant About page](https://assistant.stability.ai/readme).
    
- How can I cancel my subscription?
    
    Once you login, click the top right button that shows the first letter of your email address. Then click “*Manage Subscription*,” and on the top right, click “*Cancel plan.*” Confirm your cancellation by clicking “*Cancel plan*.”
