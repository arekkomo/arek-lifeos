## Summary

JavisGPT is a large multimodal language model capable of both understanding and generating synchronized audio-video content. It unifies audio, visual, and language modalities, enabling tasks like audiovisual QA, text-to-video generation, and synchronized sound synthesis.

## Features

- Unified multimodal (audio, video, text) LLM
- SyncFusion module for audiovisual temporal fusion
- JAV-DiT generator for synchronized video generation
- Instruction-tuned with JavisInst-Omni dataset
- Apache-2.0 licensed and NeurIPS 2025 Spotlight project

## Use Cases

- Audiovisual comprehension and instruction-based generation
- Text-to-sounding video synthesis
- Research on large multimodal language models
