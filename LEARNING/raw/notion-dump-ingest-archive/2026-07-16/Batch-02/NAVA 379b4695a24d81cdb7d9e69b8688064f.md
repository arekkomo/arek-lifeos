## Capabilities

- Joint video and audio generation with native audio-visual synchronization

- Frame-level audio-video coherence for realistic scene generation
- End-to-end multimodal generation pipeline
- Audio-visual content creation without separate sync stages

- Generate fully synchronized audio-visual scenes for content creation
- Create scene assets with natural audio-visual coordination
- Rapid prototyping of audio-visual sequences for pre-vis workflows
- Reduce post-production audio sync requirements in pipelines

