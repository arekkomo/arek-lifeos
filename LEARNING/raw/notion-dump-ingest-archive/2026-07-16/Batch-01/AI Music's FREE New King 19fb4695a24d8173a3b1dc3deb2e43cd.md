### **Summary**

*The AI music space, previously dominated by Yudo and Suo, has a new challenger: Riffusion. Initially launched in 2022, Riffusion has returned in beta form, offering free access (for now) with unlimited song generations. The platform allows users to generate entire songs based on text prompts, covering various genres, and even supports advanced customization through prompt strength sliders. Users can also upload their own recordings for remixing, extending, or transforming into different styles. Additionally, a personalization feature is in development to tailor music generation to user preferences.*

---

### **Key Takeaways**

Riffusion is back in beta and currently free, allowing unlimited AI-generated music creations.  

It generates full songs from text prompts, covering multiple genres with impressive vocal and instrumental quality.  

Advanced customization options enable users to blend musical styles and control the influence of different elements.  

Users can upload existing recordings to remix, extend, or transform them into new styles.  

A personalization feature is in development to tailor music generation based on user preferences.  

The free access is temporary, so users are encouraged to take advantage of it while it lasts.
