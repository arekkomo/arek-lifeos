## Summary

Alive is a multimodal generation framework that produces temporally aligned video and audio from text or images. It introduces novel cross-modal attention and temporal encoding techniques to improve synchronization and quality, pushing beyond traditional text-to-video systems.

## Features

- Joint audio-video generation (text/image to video+audio)
- Temporal Audio-Visual Cross-Attention (TA-CrossAttn)
- UniTemp-RoPE for temporal alignment
- Cascaded AV refiner for high-resolution output
- Large-scale multimodal training pipeline
- Benchmark performance competitive with SOTA models

## Use Cases

- AI video generation with synchronized sound
- Character animation with dialogue or effects
- Content creation for media and marketing
- Research in multimodal generative AI
- Prototyping advanced video generation systems
