### **Creating Advanced Scenes with MidJourney and Adobe Tools**

### **Introduction**

- The video provides a complete course on creating cinematic scenes using:
    - **MidJourney** for image generation.
    - Adobe tools: **Photoshop**, **After Effects**, and **Premiere Pro**.
- Targeted for creators aiming to produce professional-looking visuals for free or minimal cost.

---

### **Key Sections**

### **1. Tools and Setup**

- Tools Required:
    - **MidJourney** for generating base images.
    - Adobe Suite (Photoshop, After Effects, Premiere Pro).
- Recommendations:
    - Subscribe to Adobe Suite for maximum flexibility.
    - Consider free alternatives like Unsplash or Pexels for base images.

---

### **2. Using MidJourney for Image Generation**

- **How to Set Up**:
    - Access MidJourney via Discord.
    - Adjust settings such as aspect ratio (16:9 for landscape or vertical for reels).
- **Prompting Tips**:
    - Use specific prompts to control composition:
        - Include elements like foreground, background, and visual style (e.g., Manga, watercolor).
        - Add aspect ratio (`-ar 16:9`) for cinematic framing.
- Example Prompts:
    - "Man with sword looking up a fiery mountain path."
    - "Happy woman in a field with butterflies."

---

### **3. Editing in Photoshop**

- **Steps to Prepare Layers**:
    - Open images in Photoshop and unlock the background.
    - Use **selection tools** to separate foreground, middle ground, and background layers.
    - Apply the "Delete and Fill Selection" technique to avoid duplicate artifacts when animating.
- **Layer Organization**:
    - Foreground, middle ground, background, clouds, and other elements saved as separate layers for animation.

---

### **4. Animation in After Effects**

- **Importing and Pre-composing**:
    - Import the Photoshop file into After Effects as a composition with editable layers.
    - Pre-compose layers for individual control.
- **Creating Depth with Parallax Effect**:
    - Convert layers to 3D.
    - Adjust Z-axis (depth) to simulate camera motion:
        - Foreground (0).
        - Middle Ground (-2500).
        - Background (-5000).
    - Add a virtual camera to move through the scene dynamically.
- **Enhancements**:
    - Add moving clouds, lightning storms, and fog to enhance realism.

---

### **5. Final Editing in Premiere Pro**

- Combine rendered animations, sound effects, and music in Premiere Pro.
- Use royalty-free soundtracks from Pixabay or other sources.
- Add finishing touches like subtitles and transitions for a polished look.

---

### **Extra Insights**

- **Tips for Advanced Projects**:
    - Use Vecteezy or Storyblocks for extra graphics.
    - Experiment with animations like moving clouds or subtle lightning effects.
- **Optimizing Workflow**:
    - Save and back up files frequently in After Effects to avoid data loss.
    - Duplicate and reuse camera keyframes for consistent motion effects.
