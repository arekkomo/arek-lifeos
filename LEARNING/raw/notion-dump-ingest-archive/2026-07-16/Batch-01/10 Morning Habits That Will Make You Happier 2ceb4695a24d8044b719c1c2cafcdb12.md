Here are the 10 morning habits discussed:

1. **60-second Wakeup Reset** (1:21): Instead of immediately reaching for your phone, take 60 seconds to breathe slowly and notice you're awake. This "transition pause" increases serotonin and lowers cortisol, setting a positive emotional tone for the day.
2. **Morning Light Exposure** (2:14): Open curtains or step outside for 2 minutes of natural light. This boosts serotonin, regulates your internal clock, improves mood, and increases energy later in the day.
3. **Make Your Bed** (2:52): This small, easy win creates "success momentum" by releasing dopamine, which motivates you to complete more tasks and makes you feel more capable.
4. **10 Deep Breaths** (3:26): Before starting your day, take 10 slow, deep breaths to activate your parasympathetic nervous system (calm mode). This reduces anxiety, lowers stress, improves focus, and promotes emotional balance.
5. **Drink Water Before Coffee** (4:02): Hydrating first thing in the morning boosts mood, improves focus, increases energy, and reduces the later caffeine crash.
6. **One Feel-Good Movement** (4:40): Engage in 60 seconds of gentle movement like stretching, shaking out limbs, or dancing. This releases endorphins, improving your mood quickly and sustaining it throughout the day.
7. **Write Down One Intention for the Day** (5:21): Focus on a single intention, such as being patient or protecting your peace, rather than a long to-do list. This reduces mental overload and provides clear emotional direction.
8. **Avoid Negative Input for the First Hour** (5:57): Protect the first hour from drama, bad news, or stressful conversations, as your brain is in a programmable state, and negative input can set a heavy emotional baseline for the entire day.
9. **30-second Gratitude Scan** (6:33): Spend 30 seconds recalling something you're grateful for. This increases dopamine and rewires your mind for positivity, leading to greater happiness and optimism.
10. **The Happiness Anchor** (7:11): Intentionally do one small thing that makes you genuinely happy each morning, such as listening to a favorite song or looking at an old photo. This anchors your mood, locks in a positive emotional state, and makes your brain more resilient to stress.
