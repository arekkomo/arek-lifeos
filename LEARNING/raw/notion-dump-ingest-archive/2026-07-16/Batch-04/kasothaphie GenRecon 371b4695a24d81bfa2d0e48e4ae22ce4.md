## About

GenRecon reconstructs complete PBR-ready indoor scene meshes from sparse images or casual iPhone video by coupling Trellis.2 3D generative prior with projection-based multi-view conditioning.

**GitHub:** [https://github.com/kasothaphie/GenRecon](https://github.com/kasothaphie/GenRecon) (code coming soon)

**Paper:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23888](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23888)

**Project:** [https://kasothaphie.github.io/GenRecon/](https://kasothaphie.github.io/GenRecon/)

## Capabilities

- Complete scene meshes from 8 sparse images or monocular video
- PBR materials (albedo, roughness, metallic) included
- Completes occluded and unobserved regions natively
- 16% more accurate than 2DGS, MonoSDF, FineRecon, Murre

## VFX / Filmmaking Use Cases

- Capture a set with iPhone → PBR mesh → relight digitally for virtual production
- Generate digital twins of practical locations for VFX extension
- PBR materials export directly to DaVinci Fusion or Blender
