## Summary

ViSAudio is an end-to-end framework for generating binaural spatial audio directly from silent videos using conditional flow-matching. It produces realistic, spatially consistent stereo sound synchronized with visual cues like motion and perspective.

## Features

- Generates binaural spatial audio directly from silent videos.
- Conditional spacetime module ensures audio-visual alignment.
- Dual-branch architecture models left/right audio flows.
- Built and evaluated on large-scale BiAudio dataset.
- Apache-2.0 licensed and open-source.

## Use Cases

- Create immersive 3D audio for VR/AR and multimedia videos.
- Research in audio-visual synchronization and multimodal learning.
- Enhance videos with realistic spatial audio effects.
