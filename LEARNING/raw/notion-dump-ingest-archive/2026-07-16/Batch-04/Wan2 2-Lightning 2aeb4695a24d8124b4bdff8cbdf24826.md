## Summary

Wan2.2-Lightning is a distilled variant of Tencent's Wan2.2 video generation model that produces text-to-video and image-to-video outputs with only 4 inference steps. It maintains visual fidelity while offering up to 20× speed improvements compared to the base model.

## Features

- 4-step diffusion pipeline for ultra-fast inference
- Supports text-to-video and image-to-video modes
- Produces 480P–720P output with improved temporal stability
- Compatible with ComfyUI workflows
- LoRA variants available for fine-tuning and customization

## Use Cases

- Rapid generation of cinematic AI videos for VFX, filmmaking, or prototyping
- Real-time ideation and storyboarding for content creators
- Efficient high-quality previews for AI-assisted production pipelines

## Installation

Access via Hugging Face; use with diffusers or ComfyUI node. Requires ~80GB VRAM for full model or use LoRA/quantized variants for smaller GPUs.

## Other Info

Developed under Tencent LightX2V team; released September 2025. Focused on accelerating video generation with minimal quality loss; ideal for creators working with Wan/LightX ecosystem tools.
