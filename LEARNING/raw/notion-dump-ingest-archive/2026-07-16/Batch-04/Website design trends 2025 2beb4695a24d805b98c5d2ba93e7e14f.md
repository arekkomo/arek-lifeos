# Website design trends 2025

Here are the design trends mentioned in the video with their key characteristics:

- Interactive 3D Objects & Parallax Effects (0:02-1:34, 2:55-3:48): These websites feature interactive 3D elements that respond to mouse movement, creating a sense of depth. They often incorporate parallax scrolling, where background and foreground elements move at different speeds, enhancing the immersive experience.

[Lusion - Realise Your Creative Ideas](https://lusion.co/)

---

- Illustrations & 3D Interactive Elements (1:35-2:42): Websites in this category heavily utilize custom illustrations and interactive 3D models (like the rotating can) that follow user interaction, making the product or content more engaging. They might also include animated textures and scroll-jacking for a unique flow.

[MANA yerba mate | Energizing infusion | Made in Quebec](https://en.manayerbamate.com/)

---

- Visuals with Depth & Immersive Experience (2:55-4:27): These designs focus on large, detailed visuals and concept art, often presented in layers with foreground, mid-ground, and background elements to create a strong sense of depth. They might include subtle sound effects to further immerse the user, especially for game studios or entertainment sites.

	

[KPR | Story](https://kprverse.com/)

---

- Dynamic Grids & Organic Aesthetics (4:35-6:00): Characterized by a flexible grid structure that adapts as the user scrolls, with every element feeling alive and animated. The color palettes are often rich and earthy, and the assets used are real-world items that relate to organic and natural themes.

[Wix Studio x Pantone Color of the Year 2025 Web Capsule](https://www.wix.com/studio/pantone-color-of-the-year-2025)

---

- Image-Focused Storytelling & Show Don't Tell (6:11-7:50): These websites prioritize large, high-quality imagery and videos to convey their message, often with minimal text. They use visual narratives, sometimes with scroll-jacking to zoom into details, to "show" the product's benefits rather than "telling" through explanations.

[Experience | Mammut](https://eiger-extreme.mammut.com/)

---

- Typography as Art & Personality-Driven Text (7:54-8:58, 10:58-11:50): This trend emphasizes the artistic use of typography, treating text almost like calligraphy with subtle animations and creative layouts that don't always conform to traditional grids. Color is often used sparingly, allowing the unique font choices and text arrangements to express personalit

	

[ChungiYoo - Home](https://chungiyoo.com/)

---

- Cinematic 3D Visuals & Guided Journeys (9:09-10:57, 11:57-13:09): These websites resemble interactive commercials, taking the user on a guided visual journey with complex 3D animations and constantly changing scenes. The focus is on stunning visuals and movement, often at the expense of detailed textual information, to create an epic and memorable experience.

	

[Prometheus Fuels](https://prometheusfuels.com/)

---

- Scrolling Animations & Extended Reality Concepts (13:13-15:08): Websites in this category feature innovative scrolling animations, including horizontal movements, and often explore themes of extended reality (XR), mixed reality, and virtual reality. They blend pixel art with modern typography and use interactive cards and animated sequences to explain complex ideas engagingly.

	

[Synchronized is a full-service creative studio.](https://synchronized.studio/)

---

- Vibrant Branding & Product-Centric Design (15:14-16:35): These designs use strong, vibrant brand colors consistently across the site, from text and buttons to product imagery and packaging. They effectively showcase products front and center with clear calls to action, often using stop-motion animation to highlight the unboxing experience and product details.

[Interactive storytelling about extended reality](https://xr.noomoagency.com/)
