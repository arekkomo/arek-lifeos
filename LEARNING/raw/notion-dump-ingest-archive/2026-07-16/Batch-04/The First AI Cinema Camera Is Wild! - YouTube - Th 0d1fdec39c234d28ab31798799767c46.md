### **Introduction to the CMR M1 AI Cinema Camera**

- **Overview:**
    - The CMR M1, also called Camera One, is the first cinema camera to integrate generative AI technology directly into the video capture process.
    - Developed as an experimental prototype by Special Guest X and First Avenue Machines.
- **Design Inspiration:**
    - Inspired by the C Kodak 16mm film camera, the M1 combines retro aesthetics with modern AI capabilities.
- **Purpose:**
    - Aims to explore how AI can be seamlessly integrated into filmmaking.

---

### **Features and Technical Specifications**

### **1. AI Integration**

- Uses **Stable Diffusion** for video-to-video processing.
- Allows real-time application of artistic styles to video footage.

### **2. Preloaded and Custom Styles**

- Comes with five preloaded styles (e.g., Blooming Nature, Cosmic).
- Users can upload their own styles via NFC-enabled cards.

### **3. Interchangeable Lenses**

- Supports lens interchangeability.
- Potential compatibility with vintage 16mm film lenses.

### **4. Resolution and Frame Rate**

- Video output: 1368x768 resolution at 12 frames per second.
- Current processing occurs on cloud servers, with data sent back to the camera.

### **5. Physical Features**

- Equipped with a rotary knob to adjust AI stylization intensity.
- Retro design includes a matte box and other vintage camera-inspired elements.

### **Limitations and Challenges**
