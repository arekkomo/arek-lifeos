## About

Pantheon360 generates 360° panoramic video from sparse anchor frames along any camera path, using an explicit 3D Cache as geometric scaffold. First video diffusion model supporting 360° interpolation for long-form coherent video. CVPR 2026.

**Project:** [https://koi953215.github.io/pantheon360_page/](https://koi953215.github.io/pantheon360_page/)

**Paper:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25449](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25449)

**Code:** Coming soon

## Capabilities

- 360° equirectangular video with geometric consistency
- Arbitrary user-defined camera trajectories
- 360° interpolation between sparse anchor frames (first such model)
- Long-form video via chained segment generation
- Stabilisation: smooth camera paths from shaky 360° footage

## VFX / Filmmaking Use Cases

- Virtual location scouting from sparse 360° photos → full walkthrough video
- Pre-visualisation: full 360° scene fly-throughs from stills for director review
- Street-level scene generation for automotive/driving film backgrounds
