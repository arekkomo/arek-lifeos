## Summary

SAM 3 is the next-generation Segment Anything model by Meta AI, designed for simultaneous segmentation of 2D images, video frames, and 3D scenes. It extends the SAM architecture with temporal and spatial reasoning, supporting dynamic objects and multi-view data, bridging the gap between image and 3D scene understanding.

## Features

• Unified 2D, video, and 3D segmentation architecture
• Improved promptable segmentation with 3D consistency
• Handles temporal information across frames
• Trained on massive curated 2D/3D datasets
• Supports Gradio and PyTorch inference

## Use Cases

Video instance segmentation; 3D scene understanding; Interactive segmentation for AR/VR; Training data generation for 3D perception models.
