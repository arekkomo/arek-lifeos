## Summary

NineNineSix-AI is an AI/ML research organization developing open-source audio and speech processing tools. Its work focuses on text-to-speech (TTS) generation, neural audio codecs, and dataset preparation pipelines for audio tokenization and model training.

## Features

- Kani-TTS: text-to-speech system with neural audio codec integration
- nano-codec-dataset-pipeline: converts raw audio datasets to codec tokens
- Active Hugging Face profile with models and datasets
- Apache-2.0 licensed repositories

## Use Cases

- Building voice-over or AI audio assets for VFX or content workflows
- Researching or experimenting with neural codecs for speech synthesis
- Integrating TTS tools into filmmaking pipelines for automated dialogue or dubbing
