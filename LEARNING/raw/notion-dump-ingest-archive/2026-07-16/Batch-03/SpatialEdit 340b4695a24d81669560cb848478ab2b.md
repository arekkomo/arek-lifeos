## Summary

SpatialEdit is a research framework for fine-grained spatial image editing, focusing on geometry-aware transformations such as object movement and camera control. It introduces SpatialEdit-Bench for evaluation, SpatialEdit-500K dataset, and a large 16B model for high-quality spatial manipulation.

## Features

- SpatialEdit-Bench for evaluating geometry and realism
- SpatialEdit-500K dataset with controlled transformations
- 16B parameter model for spatial editing tasks
- Supports object motion, rotation, and camera trajectory changes
- Tools for benchmarking object-level and camera-level edits

## Use Cases

- Research in spatially-aware generative models
- Training and evaluating geometry-consistent image editing systems
- Enhancing video generation with spatial consistency
- Benchmarking editing models beyond visual quality
