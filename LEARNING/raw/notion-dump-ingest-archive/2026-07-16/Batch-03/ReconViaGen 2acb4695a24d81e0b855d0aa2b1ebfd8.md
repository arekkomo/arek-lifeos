## Summary

ReconViaGen is a novel 3D reconstruction system that fuses diffusion-based generative priors with multi-view reconstruction to produce accurate and complete 3D objects, even from sparse inputs. Developed by GAP-LAB at CUHK-SZ, it represents a next-generation approach for high-fidelity 3D recovery using AI generation and geometric consistency.

## Features

- Combines diffusion-based generative priors with geometric constraints
- Handles sparse or incomplete multi-view inputs
- Produces detailed geometry and realistic textures
- Interactive demo available via Hugging Face

## Use Cases

Ideal for filmmakers, VFX artists, and AI creators aiming to reconstruct 3D models from limited image/video sources for AR/VR, virtual production, or AI-generated scenes.
