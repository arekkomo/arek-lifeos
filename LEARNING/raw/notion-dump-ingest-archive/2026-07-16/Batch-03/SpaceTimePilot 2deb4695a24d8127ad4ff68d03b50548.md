## Summary

SpaceTimePilot is a controllable video diffusion model that disentangles spatial viewpoints and temporal motion for generative video rendering. It enables free 4D exploration of dynamic scenes by learning to independently control space and time dimensions.

## Features

- Spatial-temporal disentanglement for controllable generation
- Supports slow motion, reverse playback, and viewpoint shifts
- Temporal-warping training for motion control
- Cam×Time dataset for dense spatiotemporal supervision
- Autoregressive inference ensures coherence across frames

## Use Cases

- Controllable video synthesis and dynamic scene editing
- Research in 4D generative modeling
- Visual effects like time manipulation and novel view synthesis
