Key features and capabilities of Nano Banana Pro include:

- **Perfect Text Rendering:** It can accurately output highly dense pieces of text, even translating them into various languages while maintaining the design aesthetic (0:36-2:21). This feature is crucial for creating accurate menus, storyboards, and detailed infographics (2:24-2:42).
- **Infinite Fonts and Typographic Styles:** Designers can create an unlimited array of fonts and artistic typographic styles, even generating entire font sheets in seconds for brand-specific fonts (2:47-4:53).
- **Multi-Image References:** The tool allows combining up to 14 different reference images, enabling designers to merge diverse influences and create new, original ideas (4:55-5:20). The integration with Gemini 3 allows for impressive understanding and combination of elements from multiple images (6:09-6:38).
- **High-Resolution Exports:** Designs can be exported in up to 4K resolution, providing much finer details for complex projects (7:56-8:03).
- **Content Grounded in Truth:** Nano Banana Pro is grounded in Gemini 3's knowledge base, allowing it to research and integrate accurate data into designs, such as infographics with real-world information (9:43-10:12). It also allows for self-referencing to check and correct inaccuracies (10:27-11:23).

The video also demonstrates practical applications, such as creating:

- **Website portfolios** by combining personal images, design styles, and content (13:11-15:09).
- **Maps** by recoloring, illustrating, and even transforming real-life places into different styles (15:19-16:32).
- **Negative space logo designs** that integrate objects within letter shapes (16:43-17:27).
- **Animations** by creating variations of a design and using different frames for animation (17:28-18:47).

The video highlights the integration with **Lovart**, a design agent that leverages Nano Banana Pro for complex design projects, including brand creation across various media like social media, websites, and merchandise (19:15-19:36). Lovart features an infinite canvas for exploring designs, natural language editing capabilities (20:20-21:27), and a "touchedit" feature for precise control over individual elements and layers (21:00-21:59).

Additionally, Nano Banana Pro can:

- **Analyze and annotate scenes** to show camera and light placement for specific compositions (22:42-23:29).
- **Generate realistic images of rooms** based on floor plans (23:31-24:00).
- **Create detailed scientific infographics** with accurate anatomical labels and explanations (23:55-24:28).
- **Maintain consistent characters** across multiple shots for narrative storytelling, even for animated characters
