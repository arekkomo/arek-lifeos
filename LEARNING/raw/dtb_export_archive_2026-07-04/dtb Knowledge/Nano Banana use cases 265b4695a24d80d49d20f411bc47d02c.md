Based on the provided video, "Nano Banana" (which the video identifies as a new Google image model) can be used for a variety of creative and practical purposes. Here are the mentioned use cases:

**Image Manipulation & Editing**

- Combining people and elements, like blending two different people into one image [[01:32](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=92)].
- Removing objects, such as people in the background of a photo [[01:55](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=115)].
- Replacing objects, for example, swapping a phone with a banana [[03:04](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=184)].
- Changing locations and backgrounds [[03:13](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=193)].
- Rotating and changing the perspective of an image [[03:24](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=204)].

**Professional & Creative Applications**

- Creating professional photos from selfies [[03:41](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=221)].
- Changing outfits and hairstyles [[04:16](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=256)].
- Generating product and brand mockups for T-shirts [[13:30](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=810)], perfume bottles [[13:43](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=823)], and business cards [[14:30](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=870)].
- Designing website landing pages [[14:52](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=892)] and online banner ads [[15:29](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=929)].
- Creating magazine covers [[07:08](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=428)], movie posters [[07:26](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=446)], and YouTube thumbnails [[15:53](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=953)].
- Maintaining a consistent character across multiple images for AI-generated films or comics [[11:08](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=668)].

**Artistic & Stylistic Transformations**

- Changing the color of an object [[05:15](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=315)] and colorizing black-and-white photos [[05:32](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=332)].
- Adding text to images [[09:03](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=543)].
- Applying artistic filters, such as a Studio Ghibli style [[07:49](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=469)] or a GTA 5 style [[08:34](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=514)].

- Generating a plausible behind-the-scenes view of a movie scene [[20:20](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=1220)].

**Architectural & Design Tools**

- Visualizing landscape designs [[16:22](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=982)] and interior design ideas [[17:15](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exWEkRHmhKU&t=1035)].
