## About

PiD (Pixel Diffusion Decoder) replaces VAE/RAE decoders, unifying latent decoding and upsampling into one conditional diffusion pass in high-resolution pixel space.

**GitHub:** [https://github.com/nv-tlabs/PiD](https://github.com/nv-tlabs/PiD)

**Paper:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23902](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23902)

**Models:** [https://huggingface.co/nvidia/PiD](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/PiD)

**Project:** [https://research.nvidia.com/labs/sil/projects/pid/](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/sil/projects/pid/)

## Capabilities

- Unified decode + upsampling in one diffusion pass
- 4× and 8× upscaling (512→2048, 512→4096)
- Supports FLUX.1, FLUX.2, SD3, Z-Image, DINOv2, SigLIP latents
- DMD2-distilled: 4 inference steps; RTX 5090: <1s at 13 GB VRAM
- ComfyUI node available

## VFX / Filmmaking Use Cases

- Upscale AI-generated images to 4K/print resolution without separate SR step
- Drop-in upgrade for any FLUX/SD3 ComfyUI workflow
- Early-exit from FLUX/SD3 + PiD decode = faster iteration loop
