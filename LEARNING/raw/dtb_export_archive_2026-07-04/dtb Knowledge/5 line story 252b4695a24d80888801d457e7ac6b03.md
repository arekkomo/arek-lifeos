# 5 line story

Tags: Writing
Date Added: August 17, 2025 8:26 AM
Type: Note
Archive: No
Spark: No

---

1. Situation  (Where? What? Who?)
2. Desire (Shows the goal, sparks curiosity)
3. Conflict (Tension or obstacle)
4. Change (shows growth, moves the story)
5. Result (satisfying audiance, ties all together)
