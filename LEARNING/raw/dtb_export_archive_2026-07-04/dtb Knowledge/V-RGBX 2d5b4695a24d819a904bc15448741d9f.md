## Summary

V-RGBX is an intrinsic-aware video editing framework that decomposes video into albedo, material, and illumination components for physically consistent and controllable edits. It combines inverse rendering with diffusion-based video synthesis to produce temporally stable, realistic results.

## Features

- Intrinsic decomposition into albedo, normal, irradiance, and material
- Keyframe-based editing propagation for consistent video modification
- Physically grounded inverse rendering for real-world video
- Integrates editing, rendering, and inverse modeling in one pipeline
- Research-backed project with arXiv paper and demo site

## Use Cases

- Physically consistent keyframe-driven video editing
- Relighting and material adjustment in cinematic or real-world videos
- AI-assisted post-production workflows
- Research in intrinsic video decomposition and editing control
