# 13 Books to Read Before 30

- **Range by David Epstein:** Advocates for being a generalist and "sampling wildly" as the true path to excellence, outperforming early specialization [[00:15](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tdkvyCeU8Qw&t=15)].
- **Getting to Yes by Roger Fisher and William Ury:** A negotiation guide that frames negotiations as joint problem-solving rather than battles. The key concept is knowing your BATNA (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement) [[01:13](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tdkvyCeU8Qw&t=73)].
- **Man's Search for Meaning by Victor Frankl:** Focuses on the idea that humans are driven by purpose (logootherapy) and can endure suffering if they have a compelling 'why' [[02:10](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tdkvyCeU8Qw&t=130)].
- **Animal Farm by George Orwell:** A classic allegory that also serves as a guide on organizational behavior and how to spot power grabs [[03:12](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tdkvyCeU8Qw&t=192)].
- **How to Raise an Adult by Julie Lythcott-Haims:** Encourages personal scaffolding, a willingness to fail, and the powerful combination of independence and imperfection for self-governed adulthood [[04:07](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tdkvyCeU8Qw&t=247)].
- **The Seven Habits of Highly Effective People by Stephen Covey:** Provides seven timeless pieces of advice, including being proactive, beginning with the end in mind, and thinking win-win [[05:04](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tdkvyCeU8Qw&t=304)].
- **Strengthfinder by Tom Rath:** Suggests that success comes from accentuating your natural strengths rather than fixing your weaknesses [[05:55](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tdkvyCeU8Qw&t=355)].
- **Influence by Robert Cialdini:** The best book on ethical persuasion, laying out six principles like social proof and scarcity [[06:37](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tdkvyCeU8Qw&t=397)].
- **Chatter by Ethan Kross:** Teaches you how to manage your inner voice of self-talk, turning it into a compassionate teacher to fade away self-doubt and catastrophizing [[07:23](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tdkvyCeU8Qw&t=443)].
- **Bird by Bird by Anne Lamott:** The secret to success is not massive heroic bursts of effort, but "slow, steady, consistent progress" by taking it "bird by bird" [[08:22](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tdkvyCeU8Qw&t=502)].
- **The Coddling of the American Mind by Greg Lukianoff and Jonathan Haidt:** Analyzes three mistaken beliefs that are warping modern life and how to overcome them by seeking voluntary discomfort and analyzing feelings [[08:47](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tdkvyCeU8Qw&t=527)].
- **You're Not Listening by Kate Murphy:** Highlights that listening is a fundamental skill that must be learned, and is the simplest way to show respect [[09:38](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tdkvyCeU8Qw&t=578)].
- **Getting Things Done (GTD) by David Allen:** A productivity system based on the breakthrough idea that "your mind is for having ideas, not holding them," which involves capturing tasks into a system to clear your mind [[10:24](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tdkvyCeU8Qw&t=624)].
