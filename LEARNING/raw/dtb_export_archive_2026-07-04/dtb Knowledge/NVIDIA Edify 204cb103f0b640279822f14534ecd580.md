## What Is NVIDIA Edify?

NVIDIA Edify is a multimodal architecture for developing visual generative AI models for image, 3D, 360 HDRi, physically based rendering (PBR) materials, and video. Using [NVIDIA AI Foundry](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai/foundry/), service providers can train and customize Edify models to build commercially viable visual services on top of [NVIDIA NIM™](https://build.nvidia.com/explore/discover).

## Build Visual Foundation Models With NVIDIA Edify

Train the state-of-the-art NVIDIA Edify diffusion model on your data to generate a range of visual content types from 2D to 3D.

1. 3D
2. 360 HDRi
3. Image

### Edify 3D

Develop models to accelerate the creation of 3D assets. Edify 3D includes a clean quads-based topology ready for editing and support for 4K PBR materials with de-lit base color, metal, and roughness maps.

### Text to 3D

Create simple 3D assets for rapid prototyping and set dressing.

### Image to 3D

Quickly generate 3D assets from a single image reference.

### PBR Materials

Generate tileable materials and textures with text prompts—like brick or mosaic—for virtual scenes.

## AI Generators Powered by NVIDIA Edify

Pioneers have created commercially safe AI generators for images and 3D, revolutionizing creative workflows across industries with ongoing feature enhancements.

[](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)

### Getty Images | iStock

Advanced AI generators combine Getty Images’ pre-shot library for commercially safe and legally protected images with NVIDIA’s cutting-edge AI, enabling the rapid creation of high-quality imagery from text or image prompts.

Advanced editing tools, such as inpainting and outpainting, allow for rapid image modification. Customization capabilities for model fine-tuning, depth, segmentation, and sketch empower users to create visual content tailored to their preferences.

The commercially safe service also compensates artists and creators for allowing their work to be part of the model.

[Try AI Generator in Getty Images](https://www.gettyimages.com/ai)

[Try AI Generator in iStock](https://www.istockphoto.com/ai)

[](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)

### Shutterstock

Shutterstock’s 3D AI service lets both experienced and novice 3D creators quickly conceptualize and create assets for prototypes, set dressing, immersive environments, and more. 

Trained on Shutterstock’s high-quality licensed data, it filters inappropriate content and compensates contributing artists for each asset generated. You can integrate it into creative applications or plug-ins using Shutterstock APIs.

[Try Shutterstock’s 3D AI Services](https://www.shutterstock.com/discover/ai-features)
