## Active Connections

### Photo restoration ↔ VFX versioning ↔ editorial provenance
**Insight:** Treating GPT Image 2 restoration as a sequence of narrowly scoped passes turns a generative edit into a VFX-style versioned intervention: the unedited scan is the plate, each repair is reviewable, and colorization remains a clearly interpretive grade rather than “recovered” historical fact.
**Application:** Preserve `source`, `restored-mono`, and `restored-colour-interpreted` versions. Use [[GPT Image 2 Restoration Workflow]] for defect passes, then route only accepted frames into [[Stable Layers]] or DaVinci/Fusion compositing rather than combining repair and creative redesign in one generation.
**Date discovered:** 2026-08-09

### Generative reframing ↔ VFX set extension ↔ 3D previsualization
**Insight:** A GPT Image 2 reframe is most controllable when treated as a set-extension brief, not a generic "make it wider" request: camera geometry and lighting are constraints; only the newly visible world is creative payload.
**Application:** Build the larger plate with explicit horizon/lens/light/negative-space instructions, validate the seam, then use the approved image as a clean plate for layer extraction, compositing, or 3D exploration through [[Stable Layers]] and [[image-blaster]].
**Date discovered:** 2026-08-09
