A king attempts to mediate a marriage summit between hostile rulers while concealing an escalating physical emergency. The comedy stays dignified: protocol, performance, and framing take the situation completely seriously. His anxiety-driven gestures read as diplomatic messages, while his daughter’s attempted escape is misread as a political choice.

**Why it works:** private shame becomes public power; every effort to contain the situation creates a larger external conflict.

**Use:** a model for short-form stories in which social formality turns a tiny mishap into an inescapable trap.
