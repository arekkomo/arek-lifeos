The comic pressure comes from treating the protagonist's alarm as socially inconvenient rather than heroic. Each rational attempt to leave is misread, interrupted, or converted into a new obligation. Physical evidence from the encounter can be planted early as a mystery and paid off in the story. The final reappearance should be played calmly: the audience recognizes the danger one beat before Adam does.
