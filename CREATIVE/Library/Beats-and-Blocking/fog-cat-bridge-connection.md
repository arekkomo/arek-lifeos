In the emotional low point of a video about withdrawal, a cat interrupts the protagonist’s isolation. The reciprocal look creates a brief opening toward life without turning into a rescue or sentimental resolution. She almost reaches for it, chooses to continue walking, and the cat leaves in the opposite direction. The action makes the separation physical and preserves the project’s restrained ending.
