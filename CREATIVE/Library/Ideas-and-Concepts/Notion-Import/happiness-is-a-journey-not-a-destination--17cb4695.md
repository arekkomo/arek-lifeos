# Happiness is a journey, not a destination

### The Scene:

A traveler is standing at a fork in the road in the middle of nowhere. There’s a big, flashy neon sign that says "WELCOME TO HAPPINESS: Population 0" with arrows pointing to a deserted, empty town. The town looks desolate, tumbleweeds rolling by.

Meanwhile, the traveler looks down the other road, which is packed with vibrant scenes: people laughing, animals playing, food trucks, music, and flowers. The sign pointing down that road says:

"Scenic Route: No End in Sight!"

---

### Punchline:

The traveler squints at the empty town and mutters:

"Guess I’ll take the scenic route."
