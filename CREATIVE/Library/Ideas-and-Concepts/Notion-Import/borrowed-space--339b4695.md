# Borrowed Space

## Summary

A slow-burning, intimate song project that began as minimalist 80s-inspired electro-funk / synth-pop around 100 BPM and evolved into a slower 70–80 BPM electro-soul / synth-pop piece. The lyrical direction shifted toward deeper longing, wanting, and restrained desire. The chosen title is Borrowed Space.

## Vocal / Production Direction

- Slow-burning minimalist electro-soul / synth-pop

- Approx. 70–80 BPM

- Breathy, close-mic female vocal filled with longing, need, and restrained desire

- Sparse arrangement with warm sub bass, soft analog pads, subtle synth texture, minimal percussion

## Music Video Concept

Stylized art-house video in a pure black void with high-contrast chiaroscuro lighting.

- Warm amber key light + subtle cool blue rim light

- Dark silk and satin wardrobe

- Sculpted, dramatic hair and makeup

- Slow-burning performance with micro-movements and held eye-lines

- Repeating structure where verse scenes return and evolve, chorus scenes intensify, and all visual threads merge in the finale
