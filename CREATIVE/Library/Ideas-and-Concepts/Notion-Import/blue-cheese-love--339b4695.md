# Blue Cheese Love

## Concept

A darkly witty, slightly toxic love song built around the paradox that something pungent and off-putting can still be prized.

## Core Angle

Dysfunction is framed as refinement—the worse it gets, the more valuable it feels.

## Hook / Chorus Idea

This relationship is like blue cheese / The more it stinks, the more it’s worth to me

## Lyrics

[Verse 1]
