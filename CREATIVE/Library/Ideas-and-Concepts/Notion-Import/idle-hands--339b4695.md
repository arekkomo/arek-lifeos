# Idle Hands

Concept: The proverb suggests that when someone lacks purpose or direction, destructive impulses emerge. For a dark-pop track, the theme can center on temptation, boredom, and the thrill of doing something dangerous simply because nothing is stopping you.

Structure: verse → pre-chorus → whisper chorus → verse → pre-chorus → chorus → bridge → final chorus.

## Possible directions to strengthen the song

1. Lean darker — make the “devil’s workshop” metaphor about self-sabotage in a collapsing relationship.

1. Lean sensual — temptation framed as forbidden desire.

1. Lean rebellious — boredom pushing someone into chaos or nightlife.
