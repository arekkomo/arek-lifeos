# Pararel Universes

Scientists believe there is infinite number of palarel universes that exist simultaniesly.
We are living in one of them colectively and individually. One tiny little dot in an infinite number of possibilities. We are living in one as a collective: us>city>coultry> planet>solar system> galaxy>universe. Of which there is infinite number of variations of very little aspect of it. Our minds are a creation of one particular universe. We can only see our own unique universe and from perspective of that particular universe. But there are other universes, any variation we can imagine and any we can’t even yet imagine.
However, we humans have been gifted with a tool, an ability, a power to  travel nearby universe - that tool is choice. Every time make s choice we travel towards universe of our choice.
