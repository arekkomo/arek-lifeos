# Ask Me Later

## Chat summary

The user requested an up-tempo dark pop track in an A Phrygian-dominant style minor key at roughly 130–136 BPM with a sultry club feel, female lead vocal, intimate verses, and a whisper-led down chorus. The lyrical concept then shifted into an ironic and comedic take on two roommates living together: one impulsive and action-first, the other cautious and question-first, with each disliking the other.
