# Heritage Protocol

## Core Concept

A science fiction political thriller about Earth's status as a protected planet, interstellar politics, and the complex web of manipulation that unfolds when humanity prematurely discovers its place in a galactic civilization.

# Premise

Earth is considered a unique heritage site in the galaxy due to its evolving life forms. The Astral Council, an advanced alien federation, sends agents to protect Earth from human destruction. While humans are deemed too primitive for Council membership, they must be protected - albeit with minimal interference.

# Main Characters

## Jake

- Son of an influential human general/scientist

- Elite military training but prefers wilderness survival

- Passionate about nature and living off the land

- Accidentally discovers alien existence

- Eventually becomes central to human-alien relations

- Survivalist mindset and skills

- Conflicted between duty and personal beliefs

## The Alien Agent (unnamed)

- Daughter of Light Fraction's leader

- Elite Council agent

- Disguised as human girl on Earth

- Becomes infected with a virus that threatens her entire species

- Blamed for compromising alien secrecy

- Returns to Earth to clear her name

- Carries burden of potentially destroying her civilization

- Struggles with duty versus personal feelings

## Jake's Father

- High-ranking scientist and military leader

- Makes humanity aware of alien existence

- Becomes first human Chancellor in the Council

- Aligns with Dark Fraction after learning of alien exploitation

- Ambitious and protective of human interests

- Transforms from Earth leader to galactic politician
