# Borrowed Space — Music Video Concept & Shot List

## Core Concept

An art-house music video framed around a single brief moment: two people meet in a real-world setting, share an instant and powerful connection, and a beautiful but fragile borrowed space blooms around them — existing only because they exist together in it.

The space is warm, cozy, suspended outside time. It is made entirely of their longing and attraction, visualised as a magnetic field of golden light and distortion. By the end, they must separate — but the connection doesn't vanish. It lingers, unresolved, in the space between them.

The ending is deliberately ambiguous. We are left with the same longing desire as the two people themselves — wanting it to last, not knowing if it will.

---

## Visual Language

### Silhouettes & Motion

The two figures appear primarily as silhouettes. We read emotion and connection entirely through movement and proximity — not faces. Performance is choreographic, subtle, restrained. Micro-gestures carry all the emotional weight: reaching, leaning in, orbiting each other.

### The Borrowed Space

An abstract, dreamlike environment that exists outside normal time. Qualities:

- Soft, diffused light — like underwater or through fabric

- No hard edges — everything slightly blurred or melting

- Suspended, timeless quality

- Intimate but boundless

- Warm, cozy, comforting — but always fragile underneath

### The Magnetic Field

The central visual metaphor. The invisible force of longing and attraction made visible. It manifests as:

- Golden light bleeding in like ink diffusing through water

- Subtle distortion and warping of reality around the two figures

- Shimmer and refraction effects responding to their proximity

- Particles or light gathering where they're close, dispersing where they separate

- The field is reactive — it responds to every movement, pulse, and shift between them

### Color Arc

- Opening (real world): Normal, grounded colour. Everyday.

- First half: Monochromatic or cool → warm golden light bleeds in gradually as connection deepens

- Second half: Golden field becomes softer, less defined, more ethereal — fading gently, not violently

- Outro: Faint golden glow lingers in the space between them as they separate

---

## Structural Arc

Real world → Borrowed space blooms → Fragility creeps in → Return to real world with lingering connection

## Shot List (Working Draft)

### OPENING — Pre-Music (Real World)

Shot 1

Wide establishing shot of a real-world location — a bar or restaurant. Everyday lighting, normal time. Two people notice each other across the space. Moment of recognition. The world is moving normally around them.

Shot 2

They begin talking — a real, natural conversation. Engaged, genuinely connected. We watch them interact in a friendly, warm way. Nothing unusual yet. But we can feel something building.

---

### INTRO — Music Starts

Shot 3

The moment the music kicks in — the world around them begins to shift. Background activity becomes slow motion, then time lapse. Everyone around them moves frantically. They remain at normal speed, cocooned in their own rhythm. First hints of golden shimmer appear around them.

Shot 4

The magnetic field begins to materialise visually — golden light, soft distortion, wrapping around their silhouettes. The borrowed space is emerging. The real world fades. We are pulled into the dreamlike void.

---

### VERSE 1 — Connection Growing, Space Forming

Shot 5

Two silhouettes in the void. Golden light is soft, not yet saturated. Distortion is subtle — like heat shimmer. They move tentatively, carefully. The space feels delicate, barely holding. Micro-gestures: hands almost touching, bodies leaning slightly.

Shot 6

Closer angle on their proximity. The magnetic field becomes more visible — golden threads responding to their movement. Light pulses faintly. The connection is forming in real-time. The field reacts to every micro-movement between them.

---

### PRE-CHORUS — Tension Builds

Shot 7

Distortion tightens around them. Golden light intensifies slightly. They're closer. The space contracts, becoming more intimate and enclosed. Anticipation building. The field pulses stronger.

---

### CHORUS — Connection Blooms

Shot 8

Peak beauty and intimacy. Silhouettes fully enveloped in golden light and distortion. The magnetic field is gorgeous and tangible — warm, cozy, enveloping. They are surrounded by this force of attraction made visible. The borrowed space is now real and solid.

Shot 9

Tighter on their silhouettes. Golden distortion pulses and flows around them like liquid light. Their proximity is closest here — almost merged with the space itself. The moment of belonging they've been reaching for.

---

### VERSE 2 — Surrender

Shot 10

They've stopped holding back. Movement is less cautious, more fluid and open. The golden field solidifies further, pulsing with their movement. They move more vulnerably. The connection feels inevitable — they've accepted it.

Shot 11

The distortion is stronger, more defined. Golden light warmer, more saturated. Their silhouettes nearly merged with the field. The connection undeniable.

---

### BRIDGE — Peak Moment

Shot 12

Maximum intensity. Golden light at its brightest. Distortion at its most beautiful. They are at their closest — almost fully merged visually with the borrowed space. The culmination of all the longing. Everything feels eternal and fragile at once.

---

### FINAL CHORUS — Fragility Visible

Shot 13

Still in the golden space, but subtle flickering begins. Light wavers slightly. Distortion becomes less perfectly smooth — tiny moments of stuttering or instability. We feel the fragility now, even in the beauty. The connection is still there but precarious.

Shot 14

The golden field softens and becomes more ethereal. Not breaking violently — just becoming less certain. Moments where we glimpse the void underneath. The space is struggling to hold.

---

### OUTRO — Return to Real World

Shot 15

They begin to move apart. The borrowed space dissolves gently — golden light fades, distortion softens into fragments. They transition back toward the real world.

Shot 16

Back in the real world. They're parting ways — perhaps at the door of the bar, or on a street outside. Normal time, normal light. But as they separate, subtle traces of golden shimmer or distortion linger around their silhouettes. Faint. Barely there. But visible.

Shot 17 — Final Image

The space between them — after they've parted — still glows faintly with golden light. The borrowed space has dissolved, but its essence remains in that gap. Open, unresolved, ambiguous. Will they find their way back to each other? We don't know. We're left wanting, just like they are.

---

## Key Visual Principles

- Subtlety over spectacle — nothing heavy-handed or emotionally clunky

- Restraint in performance — micro-movements carry all emotional weight

- The magnetic field is reactive — it responds to every movement and shift between the two figures

- The space is as much a character as the two people

- Color and distortion indicate the strength and stability of connection

- Ambiguity at the end — we are left with longing, just like they are

- Everything framed around one brief real-world meeting and its emotional aftermath

- The ending is bittersweet, not tragic — the connection persists, just separated by distance
