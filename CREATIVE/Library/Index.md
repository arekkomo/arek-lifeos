# Creative Library Index

> Where your creative moments live. A living reference of scenes, beats, ideas, and observations pulled from viewing, life, and imagination — organized and cross-linked for future use in scripts, stories, and direction.

---

## How to Use

- **Record anything that struck you** — a blocking beat, a line of dialogue, a visual moment, a tone reference
- **Tag with category** so the cron analysis can find patterns
- **Update source/show name** when you remember it
- **Pull from library** when drafting — browse tags or ask the Director to suggest relevant beats
