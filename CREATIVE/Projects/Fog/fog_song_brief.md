# Fog

## Core philosophy
**Armor ↔ tenderness.** The eye-roll and the whisper are the same act of self-preservation.

## Creative direction
A woman shuts out the world after a lost love. Her diva energy is not a contradiction of her sadness—it is how she survives it. The verses and chorus stay intimate, dreamy, and close; the pre-choruses let the sass show through.
