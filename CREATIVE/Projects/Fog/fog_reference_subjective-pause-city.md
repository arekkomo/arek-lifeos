A time-freeze device used as emotional perspective rather than supernatural plot. The world is not literally dead or empty: the protagonist has chosen not to participate in it. Each held moment is caught mid-action and adds a small piece of evidence—pressure, sorrow, cruelty, or indifference—until her withdrawal feels earned. The effect works best when the performer stays contained and moving, while the city’s accumulated stillness becomes the story.
