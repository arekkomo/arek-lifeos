## The Idea

Nyala enters or moves through a room. The narrator treats it as a historic public appearance — the kind of entrance that stops people. She is composed, inevitable, significant.

Pepe runs through frame and ruins it completely. Neither of them reacts to the other.

---

## Narration Draft

```
NARRATOR (V.O.)

She had arrived.

Not with announcement — she had long since decided
that announcement was for people who were not already expected.

For thirty-seven seconds, the room acknowledged her.

And then Pepe entered.

She made no comment on this. She rarely did.
It was not, she had explained to no one, worth the energy.
```

---

## What to Film

- **Nyala** — entering a room, or settling into a chosen position. Slow. Composed. Wherever she naturally lands with some gravity.
- **Pepe** — moving fast through the same space. Any run-through, interrupt, or sudden appearance works.
- **Framing idea** — try to get Nyala in a few different framings (close, medium, wider). You'll find in the edit what works best.
- **The non-reaction** — if you can catch Nyala looking completely unbothered after Pepe's chaos, that's the ending.

---

## Edit Notes

- Open on Nyala arriving or settling — hold it a beat longer than feels comfortable
- Let the narration breathe over her stillness
- Cut to Pepe — sudden energy shift
- Cut back to Nyala. Unchanged. That's the joke.
- Music: something faux-prestige, slightly melancholy. Sparse strings or piano underneath everything.
- Lower thirds: **NYALA** / *Former Icon* on first appearance. **PEPE** / *Present* on his.
