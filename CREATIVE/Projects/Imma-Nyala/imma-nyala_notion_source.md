# Imma Nyala

> A dry mockumentary about a faded Egyptian Mau supermodel waiting for her comeback, while her chaotic Toyger adopted child accidentally becomes the real star.

Double purpose: every episode is a directing exercise. Content output = directing school.

## One-Line Premise

Imma Nyala is a fake high-end reality series about two cats who do not understand how ridiculous they are.

## Characters

Nyala — Egyptian Mau. Former supermodel. Faded celebrity. Self-appointed matriarch. Waiting for "the call." Elegant, dry, sardonic, status-obsessed. Visual language: slow camera, soft light, composed framing, dramatic close-ups.

Pepe — Toyger. Adopted child. Chaos agent. Accidental influencer. Vocal, needy, chaotic, lovable. Visual language: handheld, fast, quick zooms, vertical framing.

Figgy — Russian Blue. Recurring guest only. Limited access — not structurally necessary.

## Format

- Short vertical sketches: 15–60 sec (Instagram/TikTok)

- Mini mockumentary scenes: 1–3 min (YouTube Shorts)

- Full episodes: 7–9 min (YouTube)

- Fake commercials / brand inserts

- AI/VFX surreal inserts

## Pilot

"The Call" — 7–9 min. Nyala waits for a major career call. Pepe disrupts. Cleopatra fantasy insert. Agent voicemail (deflated). Post-credits: MeNyala perfume commercial.

## Directing Curriculum Integration

Each episode maps to a specific directing skill. Module 01: Visual Language → Module 02: Storytelling → Module 03: AI. 10 episodes mapped across 3 modules.

## Taglines
