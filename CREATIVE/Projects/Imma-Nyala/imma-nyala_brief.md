# Imma Nyala

> A dry mockumentary about a faded Egyptian Mau supermodel waiting for her comeback, while her chaotic Toyger adopted child accidentally becomes the real star.
> 
> **Double purpose:** every episode is a directing exercise. Content output = directing school.

---

## One-Line Premise

Imma Nyala is a fake high-end reality series about two cats who do not understand how ridiculous they are.

---

## The Characters

### Nyala — Egyptian Mau
**Role:** Former supermodel. Faded celebrity. Self-appointed matriarch. Waiting for "the call."  
**Personality:** Elegant, dry, sardonic, spoiled, entitled, theatrical, controlled. Status-obsessed.  
**Comedy engine:** Treats tiny domestic cat behaviour as major public scandal or career-defining betrayal.  
**Visual language:** Slow camera. Soft light. Shallow depth of field. Composed framing. Dramatic close-ups. Gentle push-ins. Symmetry where possible.  
**What she never does:** Acknowledge the absurdity. Break composure voluntarily.

**Nyala themes:**
- Former supermodel awaiting her comeback
- Believes she is still the face of luxury
- Treats the home as a mansion / estate
- Has a complicated relationship with Pepe while insisting she is a generous mother
- Gives masterclasses nobody asked for
- Has invented products, perfume, campaigns, and legacy brands around herself
- Speaks as if every moment is part of a memoir

---

### Pepe — Toyger
**Role:** Adopted child. Chaos agent. Accidental influencer. Emotional disruptor.  
**Personality:** Vocal, needy, attention-seeking, chaotic, impulsive, lovable, shameless.  
**Comedy engine:** Ruins Nyala's controlled elegance through sheer unstoppable presence.  
**Visual language:** Handheld. Fast. Quick zooms. Phone-style / vertical framing. Sudden cuts. Disruptive sound design.  
**What he never does:** Understand that he is disrupting anything. He simply exists.

**Pepe themes:**
- Accidental influencer — thinks being loud is a career strategy
- Interrupts Nyala's interviews and elegant moments
- Creates chaos during composed scenes
- Does not understand boundaries
- Turns domestic incidents into viral opportunities

---

### Figgy — Russian Blue
**Role:** Recurring guest. Not a primary cast member.  
**Access constraint:** Limited. Do not build format around Figgy.  
**Works as:** Special guest, mysterious neighbour, refined outsider, rival, cameo.

---

## The Narrator

The narrator is the spine of the show. **Calm. Composed. Slightly elevated. Affectionate but matter-of-fact.** David Attenborough meets Lindsay Duncan in a prestige celebrity documentary.

The narrator never sounds wacky. The narrator never winks at the camera. The absurdity lands because the narrator treats everything as genuinely significant.

## Tone References

- Little Britain — character-based deadpan comedy
- Catherine Tate — character specificity, commitment to the bit
- Reality-TV confessionals — the format used as a weapon
- Prestige celebrity documentary — the tone inverted
- Nature documentary narration — calm seriousness that makes absurdity funnier

**The core mechanism:** serious language + ordinary cat behaviour = comedy.  
**Secondary mechanism:** Nyala's controlled elegance + Pepe's existence = comedy.

---

## What to Avoid

- Generic cute pet content
- A narrator who explains the joke or sounds sarcastic/goofy
- Saying Nyala is "delusional" — **show it, never state it**
- Making Pepe only annoying — he must be lovable and charismatic
- Making Nyala purely mean — elegance and vulnerability must exist beneath the diva
- Cluttered graphic design
- AI/VFX as the main footage source — real cat behaviour is the base

---

## Recurring Segments

**Nyala's Masterclass** — profound-sounding lessons about elegance, fame, power, or survival. The advice is petty, impractical, or clearly cat behaviour.  
*Topics: how to enter a room, how to ignore someone with dignity, how to maintain mystery while demanding food.*

**Pepe's Influencer Academy** — modern influence through chaos. Lessons on attention, volume, interruption, shamelessness.  
*Topics: if they are not looking, scream. Every closed door is a brand opportunity.*

**MeNyala Perfume** — fake luxury perfume commercial. Overly serious, sensual, absurd. Works as post-credits or standalone.

**Cleopatra / Ancient Icon Flashback** — Nyala has always been worshipped. AI/VFX heightened insert. Externalises her self-image.

**Confessionals** — fake reality-TV interview moments. Handled via narrator interpretation, subtitles, voiceover "translation," or silent stares.

## Visual Branding

- Title-forward. Minimal text.
- Elegant but funny. Faux-premium reality TV energy.
- Nyala as the face of the brand. Pepe as the disruptive counterweight.
- Posters feel like reality-TV key art or prestige documentary campaigns.

**Tagline options:**
- "She's not difficult. She's iconic."
- "A reality show. Unfortunately for everyone."
- "Former supermodel. Current problem."
- "Elegance is not a choice. It is a burden."
- "A domestic empire. A fragile legacy. A Toyger named Pepe."

## Production Approach

**Footage first.** Film Nyala and Pepe in ordinary behaviour. Write the narration around what they do.

**Useful capture categories:**
- Nyala: staring, posing, grooming, ignoring, walking slowly, sitting elegantly
- Pepe: yelling, running, interrupting, climbing, demanding attention, entering frame unexpectedly
- Nyala and Pepe interactions
- Domestic spaces that can be reframed as luxury estate locations (living room, patio, windowsills, hallways)
- Vancouver bike/travel moments for expanded worldbuilding

## Directing Curriculum Integration

**Each episode is a directing exercise.** The project is simultaneously:
1. A content series for @imma_nyala
2. A practical directing school

Each episode has a **Directing Focus** — a specific skill from the curriculum practiced in that episode. The constraint of filming uncontrollable cats is not a bug. It is the training. A director who can find composition, intention, and story in chaotic real behaviour can direct anything.
