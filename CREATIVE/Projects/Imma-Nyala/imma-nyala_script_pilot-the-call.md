# Pilot — "The Call"

**Target length:** 7–9 minutes  
**Format:** Full mockumentary episode  
**Platform:** YouTube (primary), cut-down for Instagram/TikTok  
**Directing Focus:** Full narrative arc — want, obstacle, outcome  
**Curriculum:** Module 02 — Storytelling Structure

---

## The Story in One Line

Nyala expects the call that will restore her to global relevance. She does not receive it.

---

## Structural Arc

```
ACT 1 — Establish the world and the characters (2 min)
ACT 2 — The wait, the disruption, the fantasy (4 min)
ACT 3 — The call arrives. It is not what it was supposed to be. (1.5 min)
POST-CREDITS — MeNyala perfume commercial (60 sec)
```

---

## Beat Sheet

### ACT 1 — The World of Imma Nyala

**Beat 1 — The Opening**  
Slow, elegant establishing shots of the house. The narrator introduces the world as if it were a prestige property. Shots are composed, warm, slightly overlit. A sense of suspended glamour.

> NARRATOR: "Somewhere in the city — on a private estate known only to a few — she waits."

**Beat 2 — Introduction: Nyala**  
First appearance of Nyala. Slow push-in. She is sitting somewhere elevated and elegant (windowsill, high perch, armchair back). Not looking at the camera. The narrator speaks of her history.

> NARRATOR: "Nyala. Former face of the industry. A presence that, once encountered, left its mark permanently on those fortunate enough to witness it. She has, since the conclusion of her public era, been resting. Strategically."

**Beat 3 — Introduction: Pepe**  
Immediate contrast cut. Something crashes. Or a yell. Then Pepe — fast, kinetic, chaotic, intensely present. Handheld. Quick zoom. He looks directly into the camera with absolute confidence.

> NARRATOR: "And then there is Pepe. Adopted. Energetic. Apparently under the impression that enthusiasm is a professional skill."

---

### ACT 2 — The Wait, The Disruption, The Fantasy

**Beat 4 — The Wait Begins**  
Nyala positions herself near a window, or near a phone/device. She is waiting. The narrator explains that she is expecting a call — the call — that will signal her return to the industry. Cinematically framed. Slow and serious.

> NARRATOR: "She had been told to expect contact. From whom, she has not disclosed. But she is ready. She has always been ready."

**Beat 5 — First Pepe Disruption**  
Pepe enters and interrupts the elegance. This is the first physical collision of their energy. Nyala absorbs the disruption with maximum dignity. She does not react. She simply endures.

> NARRATOR: "The disruption was, as always, instantaneous."

**Beat 6 — Nyala's Masterclass**  
Nyala gives a brief masterclass. A composed shot of her in a deliberate pose. The narrator frames it as a formal lecture series on presence, power, or something equally impractical.

*Suggested topic: "How to remain relevant in a room that has not noticed you have entered."*

**Beat 7 — Pepe's Influencer Academy**  
Cut to Pepe, delivering the inverse. His lesson is delivered through action rather than composure. He is probably yelling at something, interrupting, or staring into camera with inappropriate intensity.

*Suggested topic: "Content creation is just wanting things louder than other people."*

**Beat 8 — The Cleopatra Insert**  
*[Placed here: after Pepe's disruption, before the agent voicemail]*

Nyala pauses. Something about the light, or a moment of stillness, triggers the narrator to offer context. This is where the Cleopatra fantasy sequence belongs — a heightened AI/VFX insert that externalises how Nyala sees herself. Brief. Serious. Slightly absurd in contrast to the home.

> NARRATOR: "She has, it should be noted, always been like this. This is not new. This is simply a continuation."

Cut back to Nyala in the house. Exactly as before.

**Beat 9 — Second Pepe Disruption (escalation)**  
The disruption is bigger this time. Or more sustained. Or it happens during a moment of particular Nyala dignity. The gap between the narrator's seriousness and what is happening on screen is at its widest here.

---

### ACT 3 — The Call

**Beat 10 — The Agent Voicemail**  
The call arrives. Not in real time — as a voicemail, a ding, a notification, or something Nyala reads. The narrator handles this with appropriate gravity. The audience expects significance.

The call is not significant.

*Options:*
- It is a vet appointment reminder
- It is a brand she has never heard of asking about "collaboration"
- It is Pepe's microchip renewal
- It is a spam call from a different country

Nyala's response is contained. She adjusts her posture almost imperceptibly. The narrator does not comment on the content.

> NARRATOR: "The call had arrived. She listened. She then looked briefly out of the window, as one does when perspective is necessary."

**Beat 11 — Nyala's Final Composition**  
One last slow shot. Nyala in her chosen position. The day ends as it began. She is waiting. She will continue to wait. There is no indication this bothers her. The narrator closes the episode without resolution.

> NARRATOR: "Tomorrow, the estate would be exactly as it was today. This, for Nyala, was the plan."

Cut to black.

### POST-CREDITS — MeNyala Perfume Commercial

*This is a standalone fake commercial that functions as both comedy and brand extension.*

Tone: High fashion. Extremely serious. Slightly surreal. Nyala is the product. Or perhaps, the product is Nyala's presence.

**Structure:**
1. Black screen, single sustained note
2. Slow close-up of Nyala — face, eyes, detail
3. Narrator delivers the tagline in one clean line
4. Logo / title card: MeNyala
5. Tagline beneath: "Because presence is not requested. It is endured."
6. Silence. Fade.

## Filming Notes for "The Call"

**What you need:**
- Nyala in 3+ distinct locations around the house — composed, elevated, waiting
- Nyala close-up sequences for the opening and the agent voicemail beat
- Pepe entrance sequences — kinetic, fast, clearly interrupting
- One clear "waiting" sequence — Nyala near a window or in a sustained position
- The Masterclass pose — Nyala composing herself as if lecturing
- The Cleopatra insert — AI/VFX to be generated separately (see AI prompts section below)
- The post-credits commercial sequence — Nyala close-ups, slow and elegant

**What you do NOT need:**
- The cats to do anything specific or staged
- Any props or costume
- Any location outside the house for the base footage

---

## AI/VFX Notes — Cleopatra Insert

The Cleopatra sequence should feel like a memory, a mythology, or a fantasy. Not literal. Not comedic on its own — the comedy comes from the contrast with the real footage before and after it.

**Suggested visual direction:**
- Egyptian or ancient aesthetic — but stylised, not historical recreation
- Nyala's colouring / silhouette referenced if possible (Egyptian Mau in a regal context fits naturally)
- Warm gold tones. Deliberate framing. Slow or still.
- Short: 8–15 seconds maximum

**AI prompt starting point:**
```
A regal Egyptian Mau cat in an ancient Egyptian palace setting, 
soft gold light, shallow depth of field, slow push-in, 
prestige documentary aesthetic, cinematic film still, 
dignified and composed, warm tones, textured walls
```
