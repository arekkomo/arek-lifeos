# Lessons Learned — Chaotic Baking

## Lyric Craft

### Less is more
- **Strip compound clauses.** One image per line beats two. Fewer words, simpler structure.
- **Fewer verbs, more states of being.** Stative descriptions (adjectives, nouns, prepositional phrases) over action-heavy lines. "What is" not "what does."
- **Ambiguity over clarity.** Every line should work on two levels simultaneously — e.g., a relationship AND a physical/chemical process. Leave agency unspecific: whose hand? whose breath?

### Pre-chorus discipline
- It's a bridge, not a new idea. If it introduces concepts or imagery absent from verse/chorus → simplify or cut.
- Aim for 2 lines max.
- Should be shorter than verses and chorus combined. Too complex = listener drops.

### Metaphor discipline
- **One deliberate reference per section** if using the motif — don't layer them. Audience needs room to feel before they think "oh, it's about X."
- Food/cooking references in a non-cooking song risk taking audience out of the emotional world entirely. Use once, use deliberately. Let emotion carry the metaphor silently.

### Enigmatic rewrite pattern
- Replace concrete actions with ambient states: `a breath held` instead of `you hold your breath`.
- Keep sensory details physical but unspecific about agency.
- The enigma works when lines could describe a relationship AND something else simultaneously — never spell out which.

## Suno / Music Production

### Style prompt discipline
- Always wrap lyrics and style in **separate code blocks**. No mixing.
- Lyrics block = sung text only (no stage directions as lyrics).
- Style block = genre, vocal, instrumentation, mood, production notes.
- Never put production instructions inside the lyric section unless they use bracketed Suno markers like `[Build]`, `[Drop]`.

### Genre shift awareness
- "Club pop" is a real genre with specific expectations (four-on-the-floor rhythm, synths, build/drop structure). When shifting direction, verify references actually match — don't just slap a label on top of existing content.

## General Creativation Workflow

### Session progression pattern
1. Draft → full, raw, lots of material
2. First pass → cut what doesn't serve the emotion
3. Second pass → enigmatic rewrite (less direct, more feeling)
4. Third pass → motif check (one reference per section max)
5. Style prompt → separate from lyrics, genre-accurate

### Acknowledge vs. act
- When told "just acknowledge — do not change anything," resist the urge to add value through edits. Sometimes standing by IS the right move. Acknowledge without rewriting.

## Reusable Principles (not Chaotic Baking-specific)

1. **One metaphor category per song.** If you layer four different motifs, none lands.
2. **Simplicity is enigma's friend.** The more words, the less mysterious it feels.
3. **Pre-chorus is a tension point, not a revelation.** Its job is to pull into the chorus, not introduce new ideas.
4. **State > action in lyrics.** "Gold at rest" hits harder than "gold settles."
5. **Genre labels mean nothing without sonic truth.** Don't name genres you haven't earned through instrumentation choices.
