# Prop Profiles

## The King's Chair / Throne
- **Story function:** The father’s formal seat of power and private trap.
- **Required look:** Imposing, richly carved, overly official; it must make the father’s inability to stand visually painful.
- **Continuity lock:** Same throne geometry, armrests, upholstery, and position in every summit episode.

## Ceremonial Handshake Measure
- **Story function:** Establishes the court’s absurd reverence for protocol before the fart breaks it.
- **Required look:** A tiny ornate ruler or official measuring instrument handled by the Master of Protocol.
- **Episode 1 use:** It measures the dignity/precision of the peace handshake.

## Kingdom Banners
- **Story function:** Make the rival courts instantly readable in a vertical frame.
- **Required look:** Two bold, visually distinct heraldic designs; avoid real-world national signifiers.
- **Continuity lock:** Each king, guard, and court zone aligns with the same banner language.
