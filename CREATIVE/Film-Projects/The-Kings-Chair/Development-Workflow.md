# The King's Chair — Development Workflow

## Stage 1 — Series plot outline (current)
Build the season one episode at a time with Arek.

For each episode, Director supplies a short description in plain language:
- What happens
- The episode’s escalation / misunderstanding
- The Daughter’s escape attempt or reversal
- The King-Father’s inability to intervene
- A hook, twist, and cliffhanger only once the plot beat is agreed

Arek then either:
- Adds or corrects story events
- Chooses what happens next
- Asks Director to propose the next episode

Only then move to the following episode. The canonical record is `series-outline.md`.

## Parallel asset-development notes
As story decisions emerge, keep separate production profiles current. Do not bury asset requirements inside episode prose.

- `CHARACTERS.md` — identity, silhouette, wardrobe, performance, relationship, continuity locks
- `SETS.md` — locations, architecture, lighting, vertical framing opportunities, recurring set elements
- `PROPS.md` — story function, look, ownership, interaction, continuity locks

## Later stages
1. Lock the completed series outline.
2. Write each episode’s 1–2 minute script.
3. Make a scene breakdown.
4. Make a shot breakdown.
5. Create assets and generate shots.

## Rule
No full episode script, scene breakdown, or shot breakdown until the relevant series-outline episode is agreed.
