# Set Profiles

## The Peace Summit Hall
- **Story function:** One contained arena where a trivial bodily failure becomes a constitutional emergency.
- **Layout:** King-Father’s throne raised at centre; rival kings positioned opposite/below him; a wide public aisle; main ceremonial doors at the far end; a visible side or rear exit for the Daughter.
- **Tone:** Excessively lavish, ancient, and serious—everything is built to make petty behaviour look historically momentous.
- **Vertical framing:** Throne height establishes the father’s formal authority but traps him; opposing king close-ups and the Daughter’s attempts to cross the lower frame create pressure.
- **Continuity locks:** Consistent throne position, banners for each kingdom, main door orientation, Daughter’s escape route, and court audience zones.

## The Main Ceremonial Doors
- **Story function:** The Daughter’s apparent exit and the Guard’s disruptive entrance.
- **Tone / look:** Monumental doors designed for state arrivals; they open with disproportionate force and ceremony.
- **Continuity lock:** Located behind/beyond the Daughter’s first route out; guard entry must redirect her toward the rival kings.
