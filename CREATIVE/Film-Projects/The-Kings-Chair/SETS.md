# Set Profiles

## The Peace Summit Hall
- **Story function:** One contained arena where a trivial bodily failure becomes a constitutional emergency.
- **Layout:** King-Father’s throne raised at centre; rival kings positioned opposite/below him; a wide public aisle; monumental main ceremonial doors at the far end; and an unobtrusive side door near the Daughter’s position.
- **Tone:** Excessively lavish, ancient, and serious—everything is built to make petty behaviour look historically momentous.
- **Vertical framing:** Throne height establishes the father’s formal authority but traps him; opposing king close-ups and the Daughter’s attempts to cross the lower frame create pressure.
- **Continuity locks:** Consistent throne position, banners for each kingdom, main-door and side-door orientation, Daughter’s escape route, and court audience zones.

## The Side Door
- **Story function:** The Daughter’s first plausible escape route—and the entrance that betrays her.
- **Tone / look:** A modest service or guard-access door made absurdly small beside the monumental ceremony.
- **Episode 1 action:** The Daughter barely opens it before a summoned army detachment storms in through it, sweeping her back into the centre of the summit.
- **Continuity lock:** Position it so the soldiers’ movement naturally pushes her toward the rival kings.
