# Character Profiles

## King-Father
- **Story function:** Host of the peace summit; the unseen source of the first disaster.
- **Objective:** Preserve ceremony, avoid offending either king, and conceal his bodily emergency.
- **Comic engine:** Inaction. Every time he might stand, explain, or protect his daughter, fear freezes him; his silence worsens the political reading.
- **Performance:** Ceremonially composed face under mounting private terror; tiny eye movements, clenched hands, rigid posture, held breath.
- **Visual continuity:** Heavy formal royal robes; seated on the throne for most of the season; never visually explicit about the fart.

## Daughter / Princess
- **Story function:** The human stake and recurring escape engine.
- **Objective:** Leave the summit and avoid marriage to either rival king.
- **Comic engine:** Every practical attempt to leave gets accidentally reinterpreted as political allegiance, then physically returns her to the centre of conflict.
- **Performance:** Direct, alert, impatient with nonsense; not a chess-master and not a passive victim.
- **Visual continuity:** Practical travel-ready layer beneath formal royal dress; a small, repeatable escape item may be established later.

## King One
- **Story function:** Rival suitor; reads the fart as a personal, public humiliation.
- **Objective:** Win the Daughter and never appear ridiculed or weak.
- **Comic engine:** Vanity turns a minor social breach into an intolerable insult.
- **Performance:** Offended stillness before theatrical outrage.

## King Two
- **Story function:** Rival suitor; instantly assumes King One is framing him.
- **Objective:** Avoid being manipulated, blamed, or outmanoeuvred by King One.
- **Comic engine:** Reflexive paranoia; every gesture is evidence of bad faith.
- **Performance:** Suspicious, quick, certain without proof.

## Royal Guard
- **Story function:** Physical instrument of accidental reversals.
- **Episode 1 action:** Bursts through the main doors and unknowingly pushes the Daughter back into the position she is escaping.
- **Performance:** Absolute procedural seriousness; no awareness of the comic damage he creates.

## Master of Protocol / Herald
- **Story function:** Treats absurd etiquette as sacred law, escalating every misunderstanding.
- **Episode 1 action:** Measures and formalises the ceremonial handshake; may name the silence after the sound as a procedural crisis.
- **Performance:** Calm, reverent, exquisitely unhelpful.
