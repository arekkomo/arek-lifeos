## SCENE S01 — Blank room, stripped down to work surface and bare bulbs

### SHOT 1
id: sh_s01a1
framing: wide shot
camera: slow push-in on a gimbal or dolly
Empty room. One work-surface center frame. First performer enters — knuckles pressing into the surface, testing it. Second performer's arm rests against the wall off-frame behind. Cool blue tones.

### SHOT 2
id: sh_s01a2
framing: close-up
camera: static, shallow depth of field focused on hands
Fingertips drag across a pale, pliable sheet stretched tight between two pairs of hands at its edges. No faces shown. Only touch as evidence. Pull taut, let snap back. Repeat.

### SHOT 3
id: sh_s01a3
framing: close-up
camera: low angle
Two torsos close together. One leans in; the other resists — a micro-shrug away that curves into an arch instead. Heat without direction.

### SHOT 4
id: sh_s01a4
framing: overhead (bird's-eye)
camera: locked-down vertical, no movement
Both pairs of hands on the same surface. Flour/dust scattered between them in random paths. One set presses down; the other pulls back. Tension visible in the way whatever sits *between* them catches light.

### SHOT 5
id: sh_s01a5
framing: tight frame
camera: static, then hard cut to black on the held breath
Both forearms brace into frame at once. Shoulders tight. A shared exhale caught too long. Cut to black for half a beat before the chorus hits.
