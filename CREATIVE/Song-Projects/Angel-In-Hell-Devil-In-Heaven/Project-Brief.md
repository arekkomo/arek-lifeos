# Angel In Hell, Devil In Heaven

## Status
**Development** — concept and lyrical world established; no lyric draft yet.

## Emotional Core
The loneliness of never being good enough, and never quite belonging anywhere. The speaker feels rejected by opposites: too tender for the hard places and too compromised for the pure ones.

## Creative Direction
A **sweeping melodic pop song**: wide, ascending choruses with emotional lift and a loneliness that remains unresolved underneath. It should feel like flight with nowhere safe to land.

## Lyrical Rules
- Convey emotion through physical, concrete images rather than explanatory statements.
- Every line must be melodic and naturally speakable/singable.
- Let rhyme, internal rhyme, and vowel flow carry the phrasing; do not force them.
- Build a vocabulary of near-fit objects and misplaced sacred imagery: gloves, beds, thresholds, borrowed halos, stained glass, smoke, flowers, rooms.
- Avoid blunt declarations of loneliness or belonging. The feeling must be inferred.

## Core Image / Title Logic
**Angel In Hell, Devil In Heaven** is a person miscast everywhere: mercy does not survive the fire, and their complications do not settle in the choir. The title should be earned late in the chorus or refrain—not used as an explanatory thesis at the start.

## Rhyme Territory
- *tight / bright / light / night / right*
- *home / bone / stone / alone*
- *heaven / seven / given*

## Next Action
Develop three chorus/refrain approaches around the title, prioritizing imagery and melodic roll over explanation.
