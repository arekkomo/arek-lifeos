# Production-Notes

## Definition
A dated, decision-oriented record of execution facts, approvals, exceptions, risks, and handoffs.

## Pipeline job
Use throughout production and post to preserve decisions and continuity without replacing scripts, schedules, or shot plans.

## Metadata
Use the shared project frontmatter: `type`, `title`, `project`, `stage`, `version`, and `updated`. Add `source:` when the document is directly paired to a script; keep the canonical file inside its project folder.

## Required sections
- Decision log
- Today’s execution notes
- Continuity and technical exceptions
- Risks / blockers
- Handoffs and verification condition

## House writing rules
- Separate observed, decided, and to-do.
- Every action needs an owner and verifiable next state.
- Link real assets rather than restating them.
- Record AI input/tool/version/selection and rights review only when material.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Material items are dated and linked.
- [ ] Decisions name owner and downstream effect.
- [ ] Continuity exceptions name all affected assets.

## Starter template
```markdown
## Decision log
| Decision | Owner | Rationale | Effective version |
## Today’s execution notes
## Continuity exceptions
## Risks / blockers
## Handoffs
```
