# Treatment

## Definition
A readable present-tense prose telling of the completed screen work: story, character, tone, world, and selected visual/sonic experience.

## Pipeline job
Build from an approved outline to align collaborators on the experience of the work before scene formatting and shot execution.

## Metadata
Use the shared project frontmatter: `type`, `title`, `project`, `stage`, `version`, and `updated`. Add `source:` when the document is directly paired to a script; keep the canonical file inside its project folder.

## Required sections
- Header, logline, and premise
- World and point of view
- Beginning-to-end narrative telling
- Tone and audience experience
- Selective visual and sonic language
- Format layer: series, music-video, or vertical behavior
- AI-native feasibility requirements

## House writing rules
- Read like the finished work, not notes about it.
- Reveal the ending unless a receiving brief explicitly says otherwise.
- Use visual detail only when it changes meaning.
- Do not turn it into an expanded outline or a script in prose.

## Quality gate
- [ ] It can be read without the outline.
- [ ] The tonal and emotional progression is felt.
- [ ] The ending and major change are clear.
- [ ] It makes no unsupported production or rights promise.

## Starter template
```markdown
## Logline
## Premise and point of view
## Treatment
## Tone and visual / sonic language
## Format layer
## Feasibility notes
```
