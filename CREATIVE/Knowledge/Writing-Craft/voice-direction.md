# Voice-Direction

## Definition
A performer-facing record of dramatic action, vocal behavior, technical constraints, and approved delivery for spoken or sung voice.

## Pipeline job
Use for narration, dialogue, AI voice, ADR, scratch performance, or final vocal continuity.

## Metadata
Use the shared project frontmatter: `type`, `title`, `project`, `stage`, `version`, and `updated`. Add `source:` when the document is directly paired to a script; keep the canonical file inside its project folder.

## Required sections
- Scene truth: objective, obstacle, relationship, turn
- Beat map
- Vocal palette
- Technical notes: pronunciation, timing, sync
- Selects and continuity; consent/identity boundary for AI voice

## House writing rules
- Direct playable actions, not emotions.
- Offer one actionable adjustment at a time.
- Use technical notes only when story, intelligibility, sync, or continuity needs them.
- State permission and intended use for synthetic voice.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Objective, relationship, and turning beat are clear.
- [ ] Directions are playable.
- [ ] AI/synthetic voice permission and boundaries are documented.

## Starter template
```markdown

## Beat map
## Vocal palette
## Technical notes
## Selects and continuity
```
