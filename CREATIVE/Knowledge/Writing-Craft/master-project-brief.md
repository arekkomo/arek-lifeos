# Master Project Brief

## Purpose

The Master Project Brief is the project’s **living context and production-control document**. Start the project here and cumulatively add its information, ideas, references, decisions, and production notes as it develops.

## Required sections

```markdown
# <Project>
## Intent and audience
## Concept and treatment
## References and visual / sonic language
## Characters / sets / props / voice direction
## Production notes and decision log
## Status and next action
```

Use only the sections the project needs.

## Writing rules

- Keep the project’s north star, practical constraints, references, and live decisions in this one file.
- Write treatment material as readable present-tense prose under **Concept and treatment**.
- Record each reference with its specific lesson, not only a link.
- Describe character, set, prop, and voice continuity with observable, production-useful detail.
- Keep dated decisions, risks, handoffs, and generation/selection notes under **Production notes and decision log**.
- Link to the separate Plot Outline, Script, Song, and Scene + Shot Breakdown rather than copying their full content.

## Quality gate

- [ ] A collaborator can identify intent, audience, format, status, and next action quickly.
- [ ] Creative treatment, references, asset continuity, and production decisions are findable in named sections.
- [ ] No standalone support file is needed to understand the current project state.
- [ ] Story-specific beats, executable script text, songs, and shot plans remain in their dedicated files.
