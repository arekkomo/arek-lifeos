# Project-Brief

## Definition
The strategic contract for a project: why it exists, for whom, in what format, under which creative and production constraints.

## Pipeline job
First document. It bounds development before story, treatment, script, or production planning.

## Metadata
Use the shared project frontmatter: `type`, `title`, `project`, `stage`, `version`, and `updated`. Add `source:` when the document is directly paired to a script; keep the canonical file inside its project folder.

## Required sections
- Identity and current status
- Intent and audience promise
- Format, duration, platform, and deliverables
- Logline, tone, world, and non-negotiables
- References with a stated lesson
- Production / rights / AI constraints
- Success criteria, decision gates, and open questions

## House writing rules
- Write decisions, not aspiration.
- Name one primary audience and a measurable success test.
- Turn constraints into concrete design inputs.
- Keep tool settings downstream; state only material AI continuity or consent constraints here.

## Quality gate
- [ ] A collaborator can state audience, feeling, format, duration, and success test in 30 seconds.
- [ ] References have a lesson, not merely a link.
- [ ] Every open question has an owner and decision date.

## Starter template
```markdown
## Identity
## Intent and audience
## Format and deliverables
## Logline and creative promise
## Reference frame
## Production reality
## Success gates
## Open questions
```
