# Plot-Outline

## Definition
A causal map of events and changes: what happens, what it causes, and how the central force or relationship changes.

## Pipeline job
Develop after the brief and before treatment or script. It tests structure, pace, and feasibility while revision is still cheap.

## Metadata
Use the shared project frontmatter: `type`, `title`, `project`, `stage`, `version`, and `updated`. Add `source:` when the document is directly paired to a script; keep the canonical file inside its project folder.

## Required sections
- Header and source brief
- Dramatic engine: want, obstacle, stakes, transformation
- Hook and inciting disruption
- Ordered beats: event → consequence → changed situation
- Escalation, crisis, climax, and resolution where useful
- Character, visual, and sonic arcs
- Format spine: timecoded track, vertical hook, or episode grid
- Continuity and feasibility flags

## House writing rules
- Make every beat observable and causal.
- Track approximate runtime honestly.
- Use musical, visual, and sound turns when they carry story.
- Do not force a feature-film template onto a song, loop, or micro-short.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Every beat changes pressure, information, relationship, goal, or image-state.
- [ ] Runtime fits the intended format.
- [ ] Hook and ending / episode pull are intentional.

## Starter template
```markdown
## Dramatic engine
## Hook
## Beats
### B01 — Event → consequence → changed situation
## Arc and ending
## Continuity / feasibility flags
```
