# Futuristic prison, long sentence

For people who hurt someone must spend certain amount of time as a panelty
Repaying the victim with its being suspend or diverting all its energy to benefit the victim.
Peoples memory is wiped out and they start fresh. New beginning without trace of knowledge of the crime and panishment. They don’t age during that time.
Two people, a man and a woman meet just after waking up from such panishment. They don’t like  each other but are forced to stick together when they found out that they had woken up from coma and don’t know world around them anymore. It’s far in the future they know 21st century only.

The world around them: utopia, ethereal and pure, clean technology. Trees like growing structures holding pods in their branches. In those pods humans serve their sentences. Through branches energy was sucked out and transported to the victim / beneficiary.

This is the world where prisons are the biggest private business which also drives other brunches like technology and ecology and overall balance.

There world where spiritualism faces technological advancement. Priests use magic to teansorfm themselves into repaying capsule to benefit victim. And others use technology to do the same. Technology is dominating as spiritualism is harder to master or understand.

In their serch for answers they discover together that they too got released from very long like centuries long sentence.
They must had done something bad because the panishment was long. So they had to hurt someone really bad. But they don’t know if it was through magic because they are bad people or through technology.
Soon before they find out that technology can be used to punish innocent people. They assume they are good people so they must have been framed. Maybe disposed? Why? What did they do that ended up being framed? Were they just random picking or were they inconvenient? And someone frame them to get rid of them?

But maybe it’s magic - and true is that they are bad people. What was that terrible that they did.

Or what if one of them was by magic and one of them was by technology. One of them is innocent and one is evil and got under spell of spiritual being.
Suddenly they get tottaly districted by the discovery that they meeting wasn’t an accident. They were set up to meet. But by whom? And why?
This is where we look into the characters relationship. Are they getting to like each other or start mistrust one another.  Both but balance is fragile. But liking and distrusting balancing each other so much that nothing is happening.
It can go eaither way.
The chapter ends where they have to make decision either or. Should they choose distrust?

What if they chose to trust another and that turns to be evil person?
