# LIKE... US? — Yumi Kant

## Chat summary

The session started with a request for lyrics for an up-tempo dark pop track in an A Phrygian-dominant-style minor key, with a sultry club vibe, whisper-style chorus, and themes of desire, adrenaline, and no-strings-attached hookups.

The first lyric pass framed the song as two broken souls pulled toward each other but too damaged by life to truly connect. The title used was “WHISPER DAMAGE.”

The concept then shifted to the same musical setting but with a completely different narrator: an oblivious, dumb, entitled valley girl trying to navigate a messy, hit-and-miss relationship. That version became “LIKE... US?”, written with bratty, nonchalant, slightly clueless energy.

The discussion then moved into artist identity and commercial positioning. Multiple artist names were explored, first ironic and satirical, then more commercially viable. After several iterations, Yumi Kant was selected as the strongest fit.

## Brand direction for Yumi Kant

- Detached, polished, emotionally unavailable

- Playful, bratty, premium-pop presentation

- Message: "you need me, but I don’t need you"

- Visual world: dark pop minimalism, club-night mood, fashion editorial coldness, whisper-pop intimacy
