# Scalpel to the Dancefloor

Concept: High-energy, chopped-vocal hip hop track about Jean: precise eye surgeon in daylight, fierce club queen after dark. The tone is playful, sharp, campy, and built for rhythmic vocal cuts.

Core Angle: Double life contrast: surgical precision by day, dancefloor domination by night. Jean is fifty, bitter, iconic, and fully in command.

Hook / Chorus Idea: Work. Serve. Snap it clear. Main event energy with crash-style ad libs and short, repeatable phrases.

Lyrics:

[Intro]

(Crash! Crash!) Clear the sight. (Ha!)

Laser steady. (Crash!)

Fifty deep. Bitter sweet. (Uh!)

[Verse]

Scalpel clean, no delay

Fix your eyes, then fade away

Scrubs off. Lights on.

Bass talk. Shoes on.

Don’t blink. Too sharp.

[Chorus – Repetitive Hook]

Work. (Crash!)

Serve. (Crash!)

Snap it clear. (Ha!)

I’m the main event.

Slide. Pop. (Glass break FX)

[Drop]

(Heavy Bass)

Ha! Ha! Ha! Ha!

Queen.

Ha! Ha! Ha! Ha!

Check the footwork.

Structure Notes: Short, repetitive sections designed for chopped vocal phrasing and rhythmic delivery. Best suited to a high-energy beat with punchy drops and 90s club references.

Development Notes: Potential expansion: add more 90s dance references like Running Man, Roger Rabbit, or Cabbage Patch in a second verse. Keep lines short and percussive for vocal slicing.
