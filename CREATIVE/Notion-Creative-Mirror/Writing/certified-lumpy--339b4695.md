# Certified Lumpy

## Concept

A dirty, offensive but funny comedy song built around the repeated phrase "lumpy dick". The tone is absurd, self-aware, and exaggerated rather than explicit. The humor comes from metaphor, escalation, and treating the phrase like a bizarre badge of honor.

## Core angle

Instead of playing it straight, the song turns embarrassment into swagger. The joke is that something awkward and ugly gets framed like a unique selling point.

## Hook ideas

- Got that lumpy dick, can’t ignore

- Like a dirt road knockin’ at your door

- Every bump like a story untold

- Yeah it’s weird but it’s bold
