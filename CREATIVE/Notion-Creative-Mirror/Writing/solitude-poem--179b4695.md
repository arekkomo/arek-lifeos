# Solitude Poem

After a week of chaos, noise's release,
In solitude's embrace, I find my peace.
Isolation's gift, a tranquil retreat,
Where my soul finds solace, in silence sweet.

No more bustling crowds, no demands to meet,
Just the whispers of nature, a rhythm to greet.
In this quiet haven, I'm finally free,
To unwind, reflect, and just simply be.

Embracing solitude, a healing balm,
I rediscover myself, with gentle calm.
The chaos may return, life's ebb and flow,
But in these moments, I find strength to grow.
