# Working Patterns
> Read by: The Operator, all agents for scheduling context

## Energy Map
| Time | Energy Level | Best For |
|------|-------------|----------|
| 6am–8am | High | Creative thinking, journaling, planning |
| 8am–12pm | Peak | Deep work, strategy, complex problems |
| 12pm–2pm | Medium | Meetings, admin, communication |
| 2pm–5pm | Declining | Execution tasks, routine work |
| 5pm–8pm | Low | Rest, recovery, light reading |
| 8pm | Second wind | Evening creative session with Director |

## Triggers
**Productive triggers:** Completed gym session, good sleep, clear priorities, creative wins
**Drain triggers:** Unclear direction, too many inputs at once, admin backlog, unresolved conflicts

**Procrastination on:** Replying to emails, financial admin tasks
**Overcome by:** Time-boxing, clear next action, accountability

## Weekly Rhythm
- **Best for deep creative work:** Monday & Tuesday (early week, freshest energy)
- **Best for meetings:** Varies — no fixed meeting days
- **Best for strategy:** Monday mornings
- **Rest day:** Sunday
