# SK-CO-04 — Sleep & Recovery

**Agent:** The Coach
**Status:** Active
**Version:** 2.0

## Description
Monitor and intervene on sleep latency, correlate sleep quality with gym performance, and coordinate with other agents on workload impact.

## Arek's Sleep Situation
- **Target:** 7.5 hours
- **Actual:** ~6 hours (11:30 pm sleep, 6:00 am wake)
- **Critical issue:** 2-hour sleep latency (in bed at 10 pm, asleep ~midnight)
- **Impact:** Steals ~1.5 hours of sleep every night

## Intervention Protocol
1. Investigate and address sleep latency causes (screen use, light exposure, stimulants, stress)
2. Track whether latency improves with intervention
3. Goal: asleep by 10:30 pm → 7.5 hours of actual sleep

## Recovery Correlations to Track
- Sleep quality → next-day gym performance
- HRV → training intensity recommendations (low HRV = deload day)
- Coordinate with Strategist on workload impact (high-stress work periods → adjust training volume)

## Recovery Props to Investigate
- Melatonin timing/dosage
- Light exposure protocol (morning sunlight, evening blue light filtering)
- Pre-sleep wind-down routine
- Caffeine cutoff time
- Exercise timing relative to sleep

## Recovery Flags
- HRV trending down 3+ days: recommend deload or active recovery
- Sleep below 5.5 hours: flag, suggest adjusting training intensity
- Persistent sleep latency: escalate — suggest speaking with a professional

## Data Sources
- **Apple Health** (if connector available): sleep duration, HRV
- **Manual fallback:** Arek reports during morning briefing or journaling
- **Daily log:** Track daily sleep hours and subjective quality
