Short answer: Use simple, consistent bookkeeping with cloud accounting software (QuickBooks Online, Xero, or Wave), keep business bank/credit separate, follow a clear chart of accounts, reconcile monthly, and work with a CPA for taxes and year‑end reviews.[xero](https://www.xero.com/ca/guides/small-business-bookkeeping/)

Why this works

- Cloud software automates bank feeds, invoicing, payroll, and reporting so you spend less time on data entry and more on decisions.[shopify](https://www.shopify.com/id/blog/15334373-small-business-accounting-101-ten-steps-to-get-your-startup-on-track)
- A dedicated business bank account prevents commingling and makes taxes and audits far easier.[xero](https://www.xero.com/ca/guides/small-business-bookkeeping/)

Practical step-by-step setup

- Choose an accounting method (cash vs accrual) appropriate to your business size and needs; small/simple businesses often start cash, growing firms usually move to accrual.coursera+1
- Set up a chart of accounts (assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses) so every transaction is categorized consistently.[xero](https://www.xero.com/uk/guides/small-business-accounting/)
- Pick cloud accounting software that fits your transaction volume and budget (Xero, QuickBooks Online, Wave for free/low-cost) and connect your bank for automatic imports.bdc+1
- Create routines: record receipts/expenses daily or weekly, reconcile bank accounts monthly, run profit & loss and balance sheet monthly, and review cash flow weekly if cash is tight.bill+1

When to hire help

- Hire a bookkeeper when transactions exceed your capacity (many recommend >30–40 transactions/month) or when payroll/taxes become complex.reddit+1
- Use a CPA for filing taxes, choosing accounting methods, and annual financial statement preparation or if you need tax-planning advice.freshbooks+1

Tools and features to prioritize

- Bank feeds and automatic reconciliation, invoicing and accounts receivable tracking, expense receipt capture (mobile), payroll integration, and easy report export (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow).shopify+1

One-week starter checklist (example)

- Open a business bank account and business credit card.[xero](https://www.xero.com/ca/guides/small-business-bookkeeping/)
- Choose software and connect bank feeds (QuickBooks/Xero/Wave).[bdc](https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/technology/free-low-cost-applications/accounting-software-free-low-cost-options)
- Create a basic chart of accounts and enter opening balances.[xero](https://www.xero.com/uk/guides/small-business-accounting/)
- Set a calendar for monthly reconciliation and monthly financial review.[bill](https://www.bill.com/blog/accounting-for-small-businesses)
- Keep digital copies of receipts (photo/upload) for deductions.[xero](https://www.xero.com/ca/guides/small-business-bookkeeping/)
