# CHS — Small Business Accounting Setup

> **Context:** Arek is setting up accounting for CHS (Creative Home Solutions). This note covers best practices and a starter checklist from a Perplexity research capture (2026-05-13).

---

## Recommended Approach

**Short answer:** Use cloud accounting software (QuickBooks Online, Xero, or Wave for free), keep business finances strictly separate from personal, follow a chart of accounts, reconcile monthly, work with a CPA for taxes.

---

## Software Options

| Tool | Best For | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Wave** | Very small/starting businesses | Free |
| **QuickBooks Online** | Growth-phase small business; most integrations | Paid |
| **Xero** | Clean UI, strong reporting, Canada-friendly | Paid |

**For CHS starting out:** Wave is a reasonable zero-cost starting point. Migrate to QuickBooks or Xero when transaction volume grows or payroll becomes needed.

---

## Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Choose accounting method**
   - Cash basis: record when money changes hands. Simpler, standard for small/new businesses.
   - Accrual: record when earned/incurred. Required as revenue grows; better for financial picture.
   - Recommendation for CHS: start with cash basis.

2. **Open a dedicated business bank account and business credit card**
   - No commingling of personal and business funds — critical for tax filing and audits.

3. **Set up chart of accounts**
   - Core categories: Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Income, Expenses
   - Consistent categorization from day one saves hours at tax time.

4. **Connect bank feeds**
   - Auto-import transactions into your accounting software.
   - Eliminates manual data entry.

5. **Set recurring routines**
   - Daily/weekly: record receipts and expenses
   - Monthly: reconcile bank account, run P&L and balance sheet
   - Weekly (if cash is tight): review cash flow

6. **Digital receipts**
   - Photo/upload every receipt immediately for deduction records.

---

## When to Hire Help

- **Bookkeeper:** when transactions exceed ~30–40/month or payroll becomes involved
- **CPA:** for tax filing, choosing accounting method, annual financial statements, and tax planning advice
