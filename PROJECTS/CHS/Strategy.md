# CHS Strategy

## Strategic Arc

```
Stage 1 (Now): Get operational — website, Google, first clients
Stage 2 (3–6 months): Steady lead flow — ads, referrals, reviews
Stage 3 (6–12 months): Niche down on highest-margin services, grow team if needed
```

## Target Market

**Primary:** Homeowners in Greater Vancouver needing reliable tradespeople for small-to-medium projects.

**Demographics:**
- Homeowners aged 30–65
- Renovating older homes / condos
- Looking for trustworthy, responsive, local tradespeople
- Frustrated with unreliable or expensive contractors

**Key buying triggers:**
- Need something fixed fast (handyman)
- Preparing home for sale
- Want to upgrade a kitchen, bathroom, or basement
- Just bought a property

## Competitive Positioning

**Differentiators:**
- AI-powered quoting and invoicing app — faster, more professional quotes than most small trades
- Responsive communication (most tradespeople lose jobs by not answering)
- WorkSafeBC insured + licensed (builds trust)
- Tech-forward: professional invoices, digital records for clients

**Tagline options (to refine):**
- "Done right. Done on time."
- "Your neighbourhood renovation experts."
- "Vancouver's reliable home improvement team."

## Revenue Model

| Service Tier | Examples | Pricing Model |
|---|---|---|
| Handyman | Fixes, installations, small repairs | Hourly ($85–120/hr) |
| Painting | Interior rooms, exterior trim | Per project / sq ft |
| Tiling | Bathrooms, kitchens, floors | Per sq ft |
| Renovations | Kitchen, bathroom, basement | Per project / milestone |

## Go-to-Market Priority (Phase 1)

1. **Domain + Website** — establishes credibility immediately
2. **Google Business Profile** — local search discovery (most clients search "handyman near me")
3. **Craigslist + Facebook Marketplace** — free, fast lead generation
4. **Business cards** — in-person / referral network
5. **Facebook Business Page** — social proof, reviews
