## Design Principles (apply before writing any code)

Based on premium website psychology — see [[premium-website-design]] for full detail.

**First impression (50ms rule):** The hero section determines whether visitors stay. Single emotion to engineer: *trustworthy local expert*. One strong headline, one clear CTA, high-quality photo (team or completed work).

**Cognitive load:** One goal per section. No competing CTAs. Clear hierarchy. White space is a trust signal, not wasted space. Keep navigation simple: Home / Services / Gallery / Quote.

**Micro interactions:** Hover states on buttons, smooth scroll, form field feedback. These signal that the creator cared — which is exactly the brand message CHS needs to convey.
