## Design Specs

**Size:** Standard 3.5" × 2" (89mm × 51mm)
**Format:** Print-ready PDF, 300 DPI, with 3mm bleed

**Color palette:** Navy / charcoal + warm accent (see [[Brand]])
**Logo position:** Top left (front)
**Font:** Clean sans-serif (Montserrat, Inter, or similar)

---

## Design Options

### DIY (Fast & Free)
- **Canva:** canva.com — has business card templates, can export print-ready PDF
- Search "contractor business card" templates
- Customize with CHS colors, add services, upload logo

### Print Services (Vancouver)
| Service | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Vistaprint | ~$30–50 / 250 cards | Ships in ~5 days |
| Staples Canada | ~$40–60 / 250 cards | Same-day pickup option |
| Canva Print | ~$25–40 / 50 cards | Easy if already designing in Canva |
| Local print shop | Varies | Higher quality, faster turnaround |
