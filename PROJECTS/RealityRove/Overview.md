# RealityRove — AI VFX Production

> **Owner:** Arek Komorowski (Founder/Strategic Vision)
> **Strategist Project ID:** P-STR-RealityRove

## Vision

RealityRove is a next-generation VFX company utilizing AI-native tools and techniques to build a production pipeline that reduces cost/time while maintaining or exceeding traditional VFX quality. 

## Core Competency Areas (Target)

- **3D Scene Reconstruction & Rendering** — Gaussian Splatting, Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF), 3D Gaussian Splatting.
- **AI-Assisted Roto/Matting** — AI background extraction, alpha mattes, rotoscoping.
- **AI Motion Tracking / Deformation** — AI-driven optical flow, morphing mesh tracking.
- **Generative Set Extensions & Environments** — AI-generated matte paintings, environment tiles.
- **Procedural / AI-Driven FX** — Fur, hair, volumetrics, fluids, particles driven by AI or neural weights.

## Current Status

- **Research Phase.** Actively evaluating emerging tools (Boris FX Mosaic, BfxSuite, and other next-gen AI VFX tooling).
- **Pipeline Development** — mapping out which tools can be integrated into a realistic shot day one pipeline.

## Strategic Notes

- **Target Market:** Indie/low-mid budget VFX, advertising, film.
- **Key Advantage:** Speed + Cost reduction via AI tools vs traditional manual roto/track/compositing.
- **Competitive Threat:** Traditional CG houses adopting AI. RealityRove wins on pipeline agility and early adoption of AI.
