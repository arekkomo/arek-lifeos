## Content Pillars (Draft)

*These are hypotheses — not confirmed directions. To be validated against the business model.*

### Pillar 1 — AI Filmmaking Process
**What:** Behind-the-scenes of making AI-generated content — shot breakdowns, tool walkthroughs, directing decisions
**Why:** Builds Arek's profile as a director/technologist. Attracts an audience of creators, not just consumers.
**Where:** Arek YouTube, Arek Instagram
**Format:** Short-form (Reels/Shorts) + longer-form breakdowns

### Pillar 2 — Aiah Syn Music Visual Identity
**What:** AI-generated music videos, lyric videos, visual albums
**Why:** Content that serves Aiah Syn's music catalog builds out a visual brand at near-zero cost
**Where:** Aiah Syn YouTube, Aiah Syn Instagram
**Format:** Music videos, visualisers, album artwork

### Pillar 3 — AI Production Experiments
**What:** Testing new tools, comparing outputs, documenting what works
**Why:** Positions RRH at the frontier of AI production — builds credibility with both creators and potential clients
**Where:** Arek YouTube (deeper cuts), Arek Instagram (short clips)
**Format:** Demo-style videos

### Pillar 4 — Directed Short Films (Year 1+)
**What:** Short narrative films made with AI video tools
**Why:** Core creative output — builds directing portfolio, proof-of-concept for RRH production capability
**Where:** YouTube (full films), film festivals (submission track)
**Format:** 3–15 min narrative shorts

## Open Questions

**1. Which platform gets priority in Year 1?**
YouTube builds evergreen audience. Instagram builds fast awareness. Can't do both at full quality simultaneously with the available time window (evenings only).

**2. What's the first piece of content?**
A Pillar 1 or Pillar 2 video that's achievable in 2–4 evenings. This sets the quality bar and the production process.

**3. What does the publishing cadence look like?**
One piece per month is achievable given time constraints. One per week is not. What's the minimum cadence to build an audience?

**4. How do Arek and Aiah Syn channels cross-promote (or not)?**
If Arek makes Aiah Syn's music videos, does he credit RealityRowHub? Does he talk about it on his channel? Or keep them separate?

## Content Production Workflow (Draft)

```
Idea → Director (concept, treatment)
     → Strategist (does this serve the business?)
     → Director (script, shot list)
     → Execution (Runway / Kling / ComfyUI + DaVinci)

```

The Director agent owns creative execution. Strategist ensures the content connects to RRH goals.
