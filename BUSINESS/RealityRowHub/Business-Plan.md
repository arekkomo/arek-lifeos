# RealityRowHub — Business Plan

> **Stage:** Pre-revenue, concept stage. This document is the living strategic skeleton — facts locked, questions surfaced, gaps marked.

---

## What Is RealityRowHub

An AI-native digital media production company positioned at the intersection of VFX industry expertise and emerging AI production tools.

**Founding context:** Arek Komorowski — VFX Supervisor at Image Engine, Vancouver. 15+ years in high-end VFX. Building directing skills through AI video generation. Has access to professional-grade AI pipeline (Runway, Kling, ComfyUI on DGX Spark).

**Core thesis:** The tools that used to require a studio can now be operated by one person with the right expertise. RRH is that person — and that company.

---

## Strategic Arc

```
Now (2026):       Concept stage — no clients, no revenue, building the capability stack
Year 1 (2027):    First content or client revenue — YouTube monetisation or 1–2 paid projects
Year 2–3 (2028–29): Established presence — recurring revenue, defined service lines
```

Connection to Arek's personal arc:
```
VFX Supervisor → AI-enabled Film Director → RealityRowHub as production company
```

RRH is the long game. The full-time VFX job is the financial engine while RRH is built. Revenue timeline is flexible — but needs to be defined.

## Open Strategic Questions

*These are the decisions that shape everything else. They need Arek's answers before the business plan can be built out.*

**1. What does RRH actually sell?**
- Option A: Original content (YouTube, streaming pitches, short films)
- Option B: Production services (clients hire RRH to make content for them)
- Option C: Licensing (sell AI-generated assets, templates, prompts)
- Option D: Hybrid — own content builds the brand, services generate income

**2. Who is the first customer?**
- Individual creators who can't afford traditional production?
- Small brands needing AI-native video content?
- Music artists (connects to Aiah Syn network)?
- Film/TV companies needing AI VFX at lower cost?

**3. When does RRH need to generate income?**
- After the condo purchase (2028–2029)?
- As soon as possible to build momentum?
- It doesn't need to — it's a creative vehicle, not a primary income source?

**4. What is the RRH brand?**
- Production company with Arek's name attached?
- Faceless / studio-style brand?
- Built around Aiah Syn content + adjacent creative work?

**5. What's the first deliverable that proves the concept?**
- A YouTube video that reaches X views?
- A paid client project?
- A short film / proof-of-concept reel?
