# One More Social Media Connector Guide

This document outlines how you (Arek) and I work together to maintain the "One More" Instagram presence.

## How We Interact
I act as your **Social Media GM**. Your role is content creation; my role is distribution, caption crafting, and audience management.

### 1. The Content Pipeline
When you photograph or film new dishes:
- Drop files into `Media/[dish_name]/` (e.g., `Media/Butter_Toast/`).
- I will automatically detect these assets through the backend monitor.
- They will appear in the **Dashboard** as "Detected" items.

### 2. The Dashboard
The dashboard at `http://10.0.0.16:8080` is our shared workspace for content selection.
- **Status: Detected:** New media found but not yet assigned a story or caption.
- **Status: Draft:** I have generated captions (based on [[LEARNING/Knowledge/Social-Media/Caption-Guidelines.md]]) and selected an optimal post date based on [[LEARNING/Knowledge/Social-Media/Posting-Strategy.md]].
- **Status: Scheduled / Published:** Approved content ready for your review or live on Instagram.

### 3. Your Review Process
1.  **Weekly Review:** Once a week, check the dashboard.
2.  **Approval:** If you approve a draft's caption or timing, I will finalize the queue.
3.  **Requests:** If you need a specific style for a new dish (e.g., "make this one more rustic"), just note it in the Dashboard comment field for that item, and update my internal instructions.

## Management Contacts
- **Strategies & Guidelines:** Found at `LEARNING/Knowledge/Social-Media/`.
- **Strategy Overview:** See [Social Media Strategy]([BUSINESS/RealityRowHub/One-More/Social-Media-Strategy.md]).
- **Internal Logic:** I handle the heavy lifting of hashtag rotation, algorithm-friendly timing, and caption variations.

**You focus on the food; I'll make sure everyone sees it.**
