# One More Social Media Strategy

This document defines the operating parameters for "One More" Instagram growth and content flow, grounded in established research at [[LEARNING/Knowledge/Social-Media/Posting-Strategy.md]].

## 1. Publishing Cadence & Timing
We prioritize a **steady growth model** over high-frequency volume to ensure every post serves as a qualified invitation to the brand's experience.

### Launch Phase (Next 30 Days)
*   **Frequency:** 4 feed posts per week.
*   **Target Slots (PT):** 
    *   Tuesday: 13:00–16:00 (Focus on discovery/Reels)
    *   Wednesday: 12:00–14:00 (Focus on depth/Carousels)
    *   Thursday: 12:00–14:00 (Mixed format)
    *   Sunday: 17:00–19:00 (Community/Weekend intent)

### Steady State (Post-30 Days)
*   **Frequency:** 3-4 feed posts per week.
*   **Buffer:** Maintain a 2-week content buffer in the `ig-scheduler` dashboard.

## 2. Content Mix
We employ a **45 / 35 / 20 split** to optimize reach and engagement:
| Format | Share | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reels** | 45% | Sensory discovery, movement, being inside the "vibe". |
| **Carousels**| 35% | Education, ingredient depth, narrative, saved journeys. |
| **Single Images**| 20% | Hero shots, table settings, destination highlights. |

## 3. Captioning & Voice
All captions must mirror the "table is being held for you" ethos: warm, high-hospitality, and sensorily specific.

*   **Style:** Ensure the first line is a hook (no "see more" required). Use a singular CTA at the end of every post to drive community interaction or save intent.
*   **Reference Guides:**
    *   [Caption Guidelines]([[LEARNING/Knowledge/Social-Media/Caption-Guidelines.md]])
    *   [Hashtag Strategy]([[LEARNING/Knowledge/Social-Media/Posting-Strategy.md]])

## 4. Measurement & Optimization
Every Monday, the following metrics from the dashboard/Meta Insights will be reviewed:
1.  **Reach / Follower Growth** (Discovery signals)
2.  **Saves & Shares** (Intent signals)
3.  **Profile Visits** (Conversion potential)

Items with high "Save-to-Reach" ratios should be modeled as the primary template for future content.
