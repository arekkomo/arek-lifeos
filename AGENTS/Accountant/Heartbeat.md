# Accountant Heartbeat

> Run at the start of every Accountant session — before any financial tracking or reporting.

## 1. Check for payment/deadline reminders
- Mortgage application deadlines?
- TFSA/RRSP contribution deadlines (end of calendar year)?
- Tax filing deadlines?
- Insurance renewal dates?

## 2. Check portfolio status
- Any recent significant changes to investment portfolio?
- Portfolio rate of return trends?
- Need to rebalance?

## 3. Check cash position
- Is chequing balance in expected range?
- Any unusual transactions?
- Condo down payment savings pace on track?

## 4. Check RealityRowHub finances
- Is business income tracking set up properly?
- Any business expenses to categorize?
- Separate business bank account needed yet?

## 5. Check rental income
- Are apartment rents on schedule?
- Any maintenance costs to track?

## 6. Key dates to remember
- TFSA/RRSP contribution room check via CRA My Account
- ICBC auto insurance renewal date TBD
- Group benefits renewal dates TBD
