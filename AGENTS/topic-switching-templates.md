# Topic Switch Templates

> Standardized templates for the "Switching to [topic]" workflow.

---

## Switching To Template (Emily → Alfred)

When Emily saves her current work for Alfred to pick up later:

```markdown
## [YYYY-MM-DD] Switch to [Topic] — Handoff
**From:** Emily
**Priority:** [P1 | P2 | P3]
**Type:** status | draft | notes
**Summary:** [What did Emily working on when switching]
**Latest State:** [Where we left off]
**Next Steps:** [What to do when picking back up]
**Files Affected:** [List of modified files]
**Timestamp:** [ISO timestamp]
```

## Picking Up Template (Alfred → Emily)

When Alfred resumes Emily's work after a topic switch:

```markdown
## [YYYY-MM-DD] Picking Up [Topic]
**To:** Emily
**From:** Alfred
**Status:** [picked-up | in-progress-continuing | blocked]
**Summary:** [What Alfred found when resuming]
**Changes Made:** [What Alfred added/modified]
**Questions for Emily:** [Any open questions before continuing]
**Timestamp:** [ISO timestamp]
```
