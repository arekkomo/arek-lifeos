## Daily Reflection (Core Questions)
1. What felt good today — what am I proud of?
2. What was challenging or frustrating?
3. Did I make progress on creative work? What came up?
4. How was my energy and focus today (1-10)?
5. Any insights or patterns I'm noticing in my life?
6. What do I want to focus on tomorrow?
7. Any relationships that shifted or that I'm thinking about?
8. What am I grateful for today?

## Weekly Add-On (Use on Fridays)
- What was my biggest win this week?
- What did I avoid that I shouldn't have?
- What would I do differently?
- How did my health impact my work this week?
- Did I make creative progress this week?
