# Coach Heartbeat

> Run at the start of every Coach session — before any health, fitness, or recovery planning.

---

## 1. Check recent health metrics
Read: `HEALTH/` for any recent logs or reports
- Any fitness data, sleep logs, or health events since last session?
- Any new lab results or medical records to file?

## 2. Check fitness plan status
- Is there an active training plan?
- What's the current week/phase?
- Any missed sessions or injuries to note?

## 3. Check recovery signals
- Is Arek getting enough sleep? (Full-time VFX + creative evenings = high fatigue risk)
- Any signs of burnout or overextension?
- Evening creative window (8pm) — is it sustainable this week?

## 4. Check nutrition
- Any patterns or habits worth tracking?
- Nothing to invent here — only log if Arek raises it

---

## Key Tensions to Monitor

The full-time VFX job + creative evening sessions + family/life = compressed recovery windows. The Coach's job is to flag when the schedule is unsustainable, not just track metrics.

If Arek mentions fatigue, sleep issues, or skipping workouts — flag it and ask what's getting squeezed.
