# A24/Neon VFX Supervisor Career Path

> **Owner:** Arek
> **Primary Agents:** Strategist (execution), Director (creative alignment), Connector (relationships)
> **Target Timeline:** 3–7 years (depends on portfolio development pace and industry luck)

## Why This Goal Matters

A24 and Neon represent the model of production house Arek wants RealityRowHub to become: artistically committed, story-first, handpicked projects with distinctive creative voice. Getting to work as VFX Supervisor at either studio isn't just about a job — it's about building the creative sensibility, relationships, and reputation that make the whole Arc viable.

This goal pulls on every agent domain: career strategy, creative alignment, and industry relationships.

## What A24 and Neon Value in VFX

Both studios have a distinct VFX philosophy that differs from traditional big-studio VFX:

| Dimension | Traditional Studio | A24/Neon |
|---|---|---|
| VFX role | Invisible polish, spectacle | Story serves VFX, not the reverse |
| Aesthetic | Clean, invisible CG | Handmade, textured, intentional imperfection |
| VFX volume | Hundreds of shots per film | Often <50 shots, each deeply considered |
| Supervision style | Pipeline manager, technical lead | Creative collaborator, artist-supervisor |
| Timeline | Fixed deliverables | Flexible, director-driven |
| Team size | 500+ VFX artists | Small, curated teams |

What they look for in a VFX Supervisor:
- **Creative point of view** — You understand *why* a VFX shot needs to exist in the story
- **Collaborative diplomacy** — You can work with obsessive, creative directors
- **Technical pragmatism** — You know how to achieve what's needed without wasting money
- **Craft sensitivity** — You care about the look and feel, not just the delivery
- **Portfolio that speaks** — Your work shows the aesthetic you bring to a project

### The Studios That Would Actually *Hire* You

Direct employment is rare. More likely paths to work with them:

1. **Be a VFX Supervisor hired on a film they produced** — they commission VFX supervisors from external studios who share their aesthetic
2. **Work at a boutique VFX studio that A24/Neon regularly partner with** — build your reputation there, get noticed
3. **Build your own company (RRH) and pitch yourself as their VFX partner** — long game
4. **Attend the right industry events and get known** — VES, AMPAS, TIFF, Sundance, SXSW

## Strategic Plan

### Phase 1: Portfolio Building (Years 1–2)
**Goal:** Build a demo reel that gets someone at A24/Neon to go "huh, this person gets it"

Actions:
1. **Create a VFX portfolio piece in the A24/Neon aesthetic** — a short film, sequence, or visual test that demonstrates:
   - Handmade textures and organic feel over clean CG
   - VFX serving story, not showing off technique
   - Imperfection as aesthetic choice
   - Minimal shots that pack emotional punch
2. **Study the aesthetic vocabulary** — learn every A24/Neon VFX film frame by frame. Build a visual reference bank in your CREATIVE/ folder.
3. **Contribute to indie films** — offer VFX supervision to micro-budget directors who have the right vision. This builds your reel and your name.

### Phase 2: Industry Integration (Years 2–4)
**Goal:** Be known in the ecosystem A24/Neon operates in

Actions:
1. **Attend VES and AMPAS events** — network with VFX supervisors who work at A24/Neon
2. **Build portfolio pieces that get festival attention** — TIFF, Sundance, SXSW are where A24/Neon talent looks for talent
3. **Build relationships with indie producers** — the people who make A24/Neon films have their own pipelines they're building
4. **Write about your philosophy** — blog posts or interviews about VFX philosophy attract the right people. "VFX as emotional storytelling" is the A24/Neon message.

### Phase 3: Direct Approach (Years 4–7)
**Goal:** Get your name in front of the actual decision makers

Actions:
1. **Submit work to A24/Neon's festival circuit** — if they produce a film you worked on, it's already in their world
2. **Pitch directly** — with a strong reel and reputation, a cold email to A24's production team is now viable
3. **Have your RRH positioned as a boutique VFX partner** — when they need a partner with your sensibility, you're the answer

## Key Tensions to Watch

1. **Full-time job vs. portfolio building** — Image Engine takes hours that could build your A24/Neon portfolio. Need to protect this aggressively.
2. **AI-VFX vs. traditional VFX** — A24/Neon may value craft and tactile quality. AI tools are an accelerant for your work but need to hide the seamlessness.
3. **Vancouver vs. LA/NY** — Geographic distance matters for relationships. Will need to travel.
