## The Core Principle

Small, consistent actions don't just add up — they *compound*. They quietly rewire neural pathways, reshape identity, and change trajectory. The key: under 10 minutes, done daily.

---

## Morning Anchors (Circadian & Mental)

| # | Habit | Time | Science |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **10 seconds of sunlight** | <30s | Andrew Huberman: anchors circadian clock, regulates cortisol + melatonin. Even 10 sec by a window helps. Do before phone. |
| 2 | **2-glass water rule** | 45s | Mild dehydration reduces cognitive performance (J. Nutrition). Drink 2 glasses before coffee. |
| 3 | **One-line journaling** | 1–2 min | Expressive writing (Pennebaker, UT Austin): reduces stress, boosts immune function, improves clarity. One sentence: a thought, observation, or feeling. |
| 4 | **Visualization minute** | 60s | Same neural pathways fire as physical performance. U. Chicago: imagined practice improves results nearly as much as physical practice. Eyes closed, picture day succeeding. |
| 5 | **Intention statement** | 1 min | Implementation intentions (Gollwitzer, NYU): stating a specific intention makes follow-through 2–3× more likely. Write or say ONE thing to accomplish today. |

---

## Focus & Execution

| # | Habit | Time | Science |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | **2-minute startup** | 2 min | James Clear (Atomic Habits) + neuroscience: starting reduces psychological resistance to continuing. Tell yourself: "Just 2 minutes." You'll rarely stop there. |
| 7 | **Single-tasking window** | 25 min | UC Irvine: 23 min to regain focus after interruption. 1 × 25-min single-focus block/day. Phone away, one tab, one task. |
| 8 | **2-breath buffer** | 10s | Before checking any notification: 2 slow breaths. Activates prefrontal cortex (decision-making) instead of amygdala (reaction). |

---

## Energy & Body

| # | Habit | Time | Science |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | **2-minute walk after meals** | 2 min | Blunts post-meal blood sugar spike, reduces energy crash. |
| 10 | **1-set workout** | 3–5 min | Research: one hard set per muscle group provides measurable strength stimulus for non-athletes. Lower bar = consistent training. |
| 11 | **Digital sunset (90 min before bed)** | Passive | Blue light suppresses melatonin. 90 min off screens before sleep preserves sleep quality. |

---

## Learning & Mind

| # | Habit | Time | Science |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | **10-min daily learning** | 10 min | Spaced repetition principle: consistent short sessions beat sporadic long ones. Compounds into expertise. |
| 13 | **One uncomfortable action** | Varies | Builds distress tolerance and self-efficacy. Can be as small as a cold rinse or a conversation you've been avoiding. |
| 14 | **Daily gratitude — 3 specifics** | 2 min | Gratitude journaling research: reduces negativity bias, increases baseline wellbeing when specifics are named (not generic). |

---

## Relationships

| # | Habit | Time | Science |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | **Phone-free first hour** | Passive | Preserves morning cognitive bandwidth; reduces reactive social comparison. |
| 16 | **Micro-connection (1/day)** | 1–2 min | Send a brief genuine message to someone. Social connection is the #1 predictor of longevity (Harvard Study of Adult Development). |

---

## Identity & Discipline

| # | Habit | Time | Science |
|---|---|---|---|
| 17 | **Name the identity** | 30s | James Clear: habits follow identity. Instead of "I'm trying to exercise" → "I'm someone who moves daily." Write it. |
| 18 | **Environment design** | Once | Reduce friction for good habits; increase friction for bad. Put the book on the pillow. Put the phone in another room. |
| 19 | **Progress tracking (visible)** | 2 min | Visual momentum: don't break the chain. Any format. Seeing progress makes continuing easier. |
| 20 | **Evening review** | 2–3 min | Brief reflection: what went well, what would I change? Encodes learning; prepares the next day's intention statement. |

## Arek's Starter Stack (pick 2–3)

Given Arek's work rhythm (peak 6am–12pm, full-time VFX job, juggling multiple domains), highest-leverage micro habits:

2. **Intention statement** — one priority per day prevents scatter across Director / Strategist / Coach domains
3. **Evening review** — closes the day and seeds tomorrow's focus
