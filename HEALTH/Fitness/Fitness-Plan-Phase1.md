## Science Basis for This Plan

### Why this works for men 48+

- **Testosterone naturally declines ~1% per year after 30.** This slows hypertrophy but does NOT prevent it. The key is higher volume and consistent progressive overload.
- **Body recomposition is achievable.** Moderate caloric deficit (300–500 kcal below TDEE) + high protein (1.6–2.2 g/kg) allows simultaneous fat loss and muscle retention.
- **Belly fat responds to systemic fat loss**, not spot reduction. Heavy compound work + moderate cardio is the most effective combination.
- **Recovery is slower after 45.** 48-hour minimum rest per muscle group. Sleep quality (you are getting ~6 hrs — this is the #1 bottleneck; see Sleep section).
- **Shoulder injury history requires mandatory warm-up** before any pressing. Rotator cuff work (face pulls, band pull-aparts) must be included weekly.
- **Lower back sensitivity** — no heavy axial loading (barbell squat, heavy deadlift) in Phase 1. Romanian deadlifts and goblet squats are safe substitutes.

## Progressive Overload Protocol

The only way to grow. Apply in order:
1. Complete all sets at top of rep range (e.g., 4×12 for an exercise written 4×10–12)
2. Add **2.5 kg** to the working weight
3. Drop back to bottom of rep range (e.g., 4×10) and climb again
4. Track every session — no guessing

**Week-to-week target:** At least 1 exercise per session should show progression.

### B12 — HIGH PRIORITY

⚠️ Tenofovir (your HIV medication) depletes B12. Combined with reduced meat intake, this is a double depletion risk.
- **Supplement:** Methylcobalamin 1,000–2,000 mcg/day (sublinguals absorb better than standard tablets)
- **Food sources:** Eggs, fortified plant milks, nutritional yeast, occasional fish
- **Check:** Blood B12 level annually minimum

### Fibre sources to hit 35–40g daily
- Oats (1 cup = ~8g), beans/lentils (1 cup = ~15g), broccoli, avocado, chia seeds, whole grain bread
- Add psyllium husk to shakes if consistently falling short

### Post-workout (within 45 min)

- Simple carb (banana, rice cake)

## Sleep Protocol

⚠️ **Current status: 6 hrs actual / 2-hr sleep latency — this is the #1 recovery bottleneck.**

Until sleep latency is fixed, do NOT increase training volume. You are running below optimal recovery.

**Intervention priority:**
1. No screens 60 min before target sleep time (10:30 pm)
2. Keep room dark and cool (18–19°C)
3. No caffeine after 2 pm
4. Magnesium glycinate 300–400 mg before bed (also addresses magnesium priority)
5. Consider tracking with Apple Health — HRV will tell you recovery quality

**Goal:** Asleep by 10:30 pm → 7.5 hrs actual sleep
