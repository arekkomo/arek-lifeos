## Notes on Strategy

- **LinkedIn** — best for Pawlikowski-tier (established, European art film world). Connect via AMPAS/VES shared membership where possible.
- **Instagram** — better for Firstman-tier (younger, social-media native filmmakers). Genuine engagement on their work before any DM.
- **Industry events** — AMPAS screenings, VES awards, festivals (VIFF in Vancouver). Aim to be in the same room.
- **The work is the introduction** — the strongest path is always a mutual contact who can make a warm intro, or being attached to a project they care about.
