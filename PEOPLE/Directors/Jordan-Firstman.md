## Career Arc

Firstman moved to LA at 20, sold his first TV show at 24 (never produced), sold a second at 28 (also in development hell). Broke through during COVID-19 lockdowns with Instagram Live "Impressions" — character comedy sketches that went viral and attracted celebrity fans (Ariana Grande, Katy Perry, Chrissy Teigen). That audience built his platform outside the traditional industry pipeline.

*Rotting in the Sun* (Sebastián Silva, 2023) put him on the film map as an actor — playing a version of himself in a meta comedy about the LA creative scene. His own debut feature *Club Kid* dropped at Cannes 2026 and was the most buzzed Un Certain Regard acquisition of the festival.
